## Platform & shell
- Global sidebar nav (Home · History · My projects · Parts & agile module · Explore marketplace · Innovations · Messages · Blog); top bar with search, profile dropdown, plan badge, Tutorial / Tour guide / Help / Report. **At phone width the sidebar is a drawer** (`sidebar.tsx` `MobileBar` + `SidebarDrawer`): below `md` a 56 px bar carries the menu, the mark and search, and the menu opens the same sidebar body over the page with a backdrop, Escape, focus held inside and handed back on close, closing itself on arrival at a new page (it remembers the page it was opened on, so no effect is needed). It used to stay open at 280 px beside every page, leaving the page 120 px — the home headline broke a word a line and the chat's canvas was off the screen (verified at 400 px: every page is 400 wide with nothing past the edge).
- **⌘K command palette** (`dashboard/command-palette`); light/dark theme (`theme-provider` + toggle).
- **Report a problem** (`components/support/report-problem.tsx`, `lib/support/`, `POST /api/reports`; requirements from User Panel V2 › "Report a Issue", Figma `HDtWAU2PSbjQKlEWgHI3ev` · `41571:176004`, look from the DS). One dialog for the whole app (`ReportProblemHost` in the root layout, opened by `openReportProblem()`), from the sidebar's Support block (expanded, collapsed rail, phone drawer, which closes first), ⌘K, and the Help menu of the PCB (its old "Contact" stub), Code and 3D editors. The form: *What's it about?* (six kinds, including *The AI got it wrong*), *How much is this getting in your way?* (Not much · It slows me down · I'm stuck; not asked for an idea), one text box whose label and hint follow the kind, and up to 3 images by Add images, paste, drop or *Screenshot this page* (tab capture with the dialog hidden; desktop only), each shrunk to ≤1600 px WebP/JPEG in the browser. The V2 title and second text box are gone: the title is the first sentence. *Send page and device details* (ticked by default, *See what's sent* lists every value) attaches the page in plain words with the maker's own project/product names, browser and OS, window, theme, a storage-full flag and the tab's last uncaught errors (`src/instrumentation-client.ts` → `lib/support/recent-errors.ts`). Send checks on press (errors under each field, focus on the first) and posts once per draft id; the route re-validates everything and keeps the report in the project's private Vercel Blob store, else `.ideeza/problem-reports/<id>/` on disk, writing `report.json` last and never twice. Success shows a reference (R-XXXXXX) and promises no reply. The draft survives closing and its text a reload; keys typed in the dialog never reach the editor behind it.
- **`/parts` — Parts & Agile Module** (`app/(dashboard)/parts` + `components/parts/parts-library.tsx`, from the `feat/parts-agile-module` branch): the part catalogue, the captured Agile Modules and the packages the maker has authored, on one section rail over a dense list — the real flow for this destination. `/parts/new` is the New Package authoring wizard and `/parts/<id>` one part with its land pattern. *(An earlier branch put a per-build bill-of-materials index on this route; it was removed on the merge rather than left as a second parts destination — a build's BOM lives where it is produced, on the build's own Parts tab at `/build/<id>?tab=parts`, which the project page links as **Bill of materials**.)*

## Add Network & Connection Map (`lib/network`, `components/network`, Figma `HDtWAU2PSbjQKlEWgHI3ev` · `44533:123190`)
Built to the spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-24-add-network-design.md` (decisions D1–D12): Figma supplies the requirements, the app supplies the look.
- **The Add Network wizard** (`add-network-dialog.tsx`) — one dialog, five steps (Setup · Method · Connect · Review · Done) with a stepper, Back on every step and a *Discard this network?* confirm once there is progress. **Setup**: the four goals (Control all via 1 App · Products talk to each other · Both — app + direct · Cloud-only data sync) and the project's products as cards with a chip derived from the build's parts (`ESP32-S3 · Wi-Fi + BLE` — MCU plus the radios the part names imply; *No parts listed* when there are none), *N selected · M not included*, and a gate (≥1 product, ≥2 for the two local goals). **Method**: AI Auto-Map or Manual Canvas and the three-questions note. **Done**: *Network created*, a line that says *saved* (never *live* — D4), the 09b variant when no Cloud Name was entered, product → role, **Close · View Network** (D2, busy *Opening…*).
- **AI Auto-Map is a real model call with an honest fallback** (`api/network/automap` + `planner.ts`) — the products (names, descriptions, parts) and the goal go to the keyless Pollinations `openai-fast` model on its OpenAI-shaped endpoint with low reasoning effort (≈12 s for four products, under 30 s for six; the plain endpoint took 30 s and wrapped the answer in its reasoning), 45 s timeout. Every proposed link is validated against the boxes on the map and the three questions' own values and dropped, never repaired (`validateAiLinks`); a hub product the AI routes others through is moved to the middle column (`arrangeFor`). No valid link → the **rule planner** (the Figma Role-rules table read backwards: sense + act → two-way, sense → reports, act → is told) draws it and the banner says so. The proposal is reviewed as a list with the roles it produces before the canvas opens (Figma 04).
- **The connection canvas** (`map-canvas.tsx` + `map-editor.tsx`) — HTML boxes (product · MQTT Broker *C* · App *A*) and SVG arrows on one pan/zoom plane (wheel pans, ⌘/Ctrl-wheel zooms at the pointer, − 100% + Fit, dot grid, legend, status line). **Select** clicks a box or an arrow's label and drags boxes; **Draw link** (L) shows four ports per box and links port to port into a *New link — not saved yet* panel. Line style follows Q2 (dashed cloud · solid direct · double gateway), arrowheads follow Q1; arrows are neutral until selected. Undo/Redo (⌘Z/⇧⌘Z), Delete key, protocol-for-new-links dropdown (all 12), How to draw dialog, *Clear and draw myself*. The link panel asks the three questions as radio pills and shows **Filled in for you** (source/target role, topology, cloud type, sensor field); **Delete this link** lives there, not in the toolbar (D3). A step cannot be left with an unsaved link on it.
- **Review fills in every field from the map** (`product-form.tsx`, `derive.ts`) — network settings (name, cloud collection, password with an accessible show/hide, cloud type, network type, frequency filtered by protocol) and **All parameters** (the five Figma reference tables, read from the same catalog the controls use); a row per product (role chip + *Ready* / *Needs sensor type*) with Topology, Master, Interfaces (add/remove), Add repeater (hidden for CAN/RS-232/I2C/SPI), Sensor type (17), Agent placement, Shares data with cloud (off while Sensor = None or no agent), Bridge (read-only, on at 2+ interfaces), Redundancy (Standby for a product with no links). A field the maker changes stops following the map (`touched`). Roles come from the arrows (Figma Role rules). **Create network** names the first thing missing (a cloud password when cloud type ≠ None; a sensor type when a link says *Sensor data*); a product whose parts name no radio for its interface gets a warning. Choosing a new Master that breaks links opens *Changing Master will remove N links* with the computed list (D7).
- **The Connection Map page** (`connection-map-page.tsx`, Figma 11–17) — breadcrumb, name, *products · links · shared cloud · last edited <date>* (no person's name — D8), the read-only map with a Network summary; a box shows its sentence and Product settings, an arrow its answers read-only (D5). **Edit map** runs the same editor on a draft, with *N unsaved changes* listing what changed (links added/removed/changed, Master changed to X, product settings, boxes moved), Cancel (confirms when there are changes) and Save changes. **Settings** (`network-settings-dialog.tsx`) edits the whole-network fields and carries **Delete network** (D9) behind *Delete "<name>"?*.
- **The project page's Network section** (`network-section.tsx`, Figma 01 / 10) — *No network yet* with **Create Network** (no kebab menu), or the network's summary, connections and products with role chips, and **View Network**.
- **The concept network becomes the project's network on Save** (2026-09-30, `lib/network/from-concept.ts`) — the review card no longer has Add Network; the network is worked out in the chat rail's Network section, and when Save makes a new project or a rebuild's next version and `ideeza:network:<projectId>` doesn't exist, it writes one from that section's links as built: products matched by name from `networkProducts`, each link's protocol through the one `radioKey → ProtocolKey` mapping (nRF24 and Cellular are in the protocol table now), the planner's boxes, `autoSettings` per product, intent p2p / ctrl / cloud, `mapSource` ai or rules — checked by `sanitizeNetwork`. A network that exists is left untouched: the Connection Map is the maker's. A link whose ends don't share a radio is left out.
  - Only when the maker added it at the concept stage (`SetupAnswer.network.added`, owner 2026-10-04) — the network is optional; a build saved without it writes no `ideeza:network:<id>`.
- **Persistence** (`store.ts`) — one network per project (D12) in `localStorage` under `ideeza:network:<projectId>`, shape-checked on read (`sanitizeNetwork`) and read through one `useSyncExternalStore` hook, so the chat rail, the project page and the map agree without a reload.

## Projects, history & community
### My projects
- **My projects** (`projects/my-projects.tsx`, `lib/manual/project-list.ts`): status is the Brief's own outcome (Draft · Private · Given · **Listed** · Paused · **Sold** · Minted); five tabs **All · Draft · Minted · Listed · Sold** (Minted holds Private, Given and an unreadable mint; Listed holds live and paused listings); a **Showcase only** toggle beside Sort (`aria-pressed`, `showcase=1`; a project's `showcasedAt` flag, not a status) that narrows the rows, not the tab counts; search, sort, 12-per-page pagination. Old links still open: `tab=private`/`tab=given` → Minted, `tab=showcase` → the filter; `source=` is ignored (the Source filter is gone).
- **Cards are one link** (`project-card.tsx`, `status-chip.tsx`): each reads the live market stores and shows the status chip (a showcased project's chip carries an eye, "Showcased"), the title, "{N} products · Saved {date}", and a bottom-aligned hint (`hintOf`: Next: review version *n* · Next: mint · Next: sell · Minted privately · Given to the community · On the marketplace · Listing paused · Next: sell another share · Sold out). No button: the whole card opens `/projects/<id>`, with a focus ring; the source, version and status line stay on the project page. Empty tabs and the no-projects state teach in one short sentence.

### Product page (`/projects/[id]/products/[productId]`)
- **Header:** breadcrumb, product name, Activity chip (scoped), build check and version select, and the page's one violet **Open in editor** (or *Open in editor · {Step}*), absent when the project is locked. There is no sample-board hint. Version history (the select, the version notices, other versions' images) is the owner's: a buyer sees the version on offer only.
- **Tabs:** **Media** (default; the video player, Regenerate, Takes, then the product's images) · 3D model · PCB · Firmware code · Wiring · Parts; on the buyer's side (Preview as others see it, Explore marketplace) then **Contributors** (read-only credit, with ≥ 1 contributor). The owner's team is the project's Team tab, and this product's edition sales are Earn › Money › Sales. A piece the build never made has no tab; a hand-made product has Media only. A demo creator without a holding sees no Firmware tab; a holder of the Main NFT, or of a Physical or Virtual NFT of this product, gets it and the downloads.
- **Rail:** **Editor** (six rows into the product's editor, the seed caption "Opens with version *n*'s …", and the one home of **Load version *m*…** and **Restore version *b***, see BUILDLOAD). The buyer's side also reads **Marketplace** (this product's listed Physical and Virtual NFT editions); the owner's edition controls are Earn › Sell › Copies of each product.
- **Per-product editor:** progress facts come from that product's own documents (`lib/manual/editor-work.ts`); the route and store are in [editor-modules.md](editor-modules.md).

### Video
- **A real browser-made clip** (`video-jobs/clip-renderer.ts`, `lib/video/*`): each product keeps takes (`ProductVideo`), at most one in use. The render draws a 10 s, 30 fps, silent file from the product's image and three on-screen lines and encodes it in the browser (WebCodecs MP4, MediaRecorder WebM fallback); the honesty line says the AI video model is not connected yet. Files live in IndexedDB; the player, Generate dialog, lightbox and global render indicator read the same job. The render toasts (bottom right, the maker's only) show two at a time, one at phone width, over a quiet *+N more videos* line; hiding or watching one brings the next up, and while they are up the project, product and plan pages pad their foot so nothing in the rail stays under them.
- **Readiness gate** (`lib/manual/readiness.ts`, `details/readiness-dialog.tsx`): a rendering video is not yet a video. One rule set gates Showcase, Sell, Give, Relist and a single edition, and the dialog lists what is missing per product.

### Mint
- **Lazy or Instant** (`wallet/mint-type-field.tsx`, `lib/wallet/mint.ts`): one shared field in the Brief form and the listing form. Lazy is a free signature and mints on the first sale; Instant pays the network fee now. The Outcome block shows the mint proof (token, signature or transaction, payout wallet).
- **Demo wallet** (`components/wallet/*`, `lib/wallet/*`, key `ideeza:wallet:demo`): six local identities (the maker's three accounts and Explore's three demo creators, Mira · Leo · Sam) with seed balances derived, never stored, in exact bigint micros. Every signature and transaction opens one Demo wallet dialog (connect, address, balance, charge estimate, confirm); the account menu's *Wallet & tokens* entry manages identities. A stored wallet that can't be read refuses every write: both the request and the manage dialog say so (not "storage is full") and offer **Reset the demo wallet**. While a transaction is pending its ✕ reads unavailable. Nothing touches a chain and every record is labelled *Testnet demo*.

### Marketplace
- **Listing** (`projects/listing/*`, `lib/market/listing*.ts`): the *Add To Main NFT Marketplace* form (Buy now or Auction, price, royalties, share, benefits) lists a project for real, once the project reaches the gating ladder's L3 (see [earn-layer.md](earn-layer.md)); the form carries the share-sale note and reads royalties as a share of resales, if marketplaces honour it; Edit, Remove, Pause and Relist, Bidding History and Close auction (winner is the highest bid whose demo wallet still covers it) run in Earn › Sell's Your project card. The Brief's Sell step renders the same fields and writes the same listing, in the order MintRecord → listing → the draft's `mintedAt`, so a refused listing write leaves nothing minted.
- **Explore marketplace** (`/marketplace`, `components/marketplace/*`): For sale · Sold · Purchased tabs, search, Type and Sort, with a *Shopping as* switch (Mira · Leo · Sam). `/marketplace/[id]` is the project page as a buyer sees it (a paused listing reads *This project is off the marketplace for now* instead, unless the active buyer already holds part of it), with the buyer rail: Buy now card, Auction bid card, Purchased Summary and edition offers, then the one messaging control: **Get creator support** for a holder, **Ask the creator** otherwise, opening [Messages](messages.md).
- **Buying and bidding** (`lib/market/purchase.ts`, `auction.ts`): a demo purchase or bid confirms through the wallet dialog (it opens on Reject) and writes an append-only sale or bid; Buy now, Buy now with …, Place bid and an edition's Buy now are `aria-disabled` while it is open, so Reject or Close hands focus back to them; an auction's top bidder can take its Buy now, their own held bid counting toward it; a Main share is never oversold; sales become Sales rows and holdings. **IDEEZA takes a 2.5 % fee** on every sale, paid by the seller out of the price (`IDEEZA_FEE_BPS` in `lib/market/fee.ts`).

### Editions
- **Physical and Virtual NFT editions** (`projects/editions/*`, `lib/market/editions*.ts`): copies of the whole project (`productId` = `PROJECT_SCOPE`) or of one product, created, listed and sold on their own in Earn › Sell, with no Main listing needed and none hidden by a paused or removed one; a track is a lazy-minted batch per kind and use (create, list, edit, add NFTs), gated by the mint, Given, the scope's video readiness and the ladder (L3 virtual, L4 physical). Explore marketplace lists a project with any live copy (a *Copies* card, or *Copies from …* on its Main card), and its buyer page offers the Main NFT when live, the whole project's copies, then each product's; buyers choose Regular or Extended, and a whole-project copy's sale is named by the project in Sales. Once the project is sold in full, its copy listings come off, the offers hide and a copy purchase is refused at confirm.

### BUILDLOAD
- **The editor opens a product with its own build** (`lib/manual/build-load*.ts`, `lib/pcb/from-build.ts`, `lib/three/from-build.ts`): the first open seeds that product's board (schematic from the build's parts and nets, then the editor's own Convert), 3D scene, firmware file and wiring parts, never over the maker's work; a refused write leaves nothing behind. **Load version *m*…** replaces a document only through a confirm, keeps a one-slot backup, and **Restore version *b*** brings it back; an import notice explains what came in and can be dismissed.
