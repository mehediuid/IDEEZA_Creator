# Features — other editor modules (Code, 3D, Preview, Wiring) and the mint flow (the Brief)

Part of the feature inventory ([index](README.md)). Moved verbatim from `CLAUDE.md` §5 on 2026-09-29. Keep it current: [AGENTS.md › Keeping docs current](../../../AGENTS.md#keeping-docs-current-required).

## Other editor modules
- **The editor opens a product** (`/project/<slug>/products/<productId>/<step>`, step `pcb · code · 3d · assembly · wiring · preview`; `manual/project-workspace.tsx`): the gate resolves the project, refuses a locked (sold-in-full) one, and an unknown product shows "This product isn't in {project}" rather than another product's board. The Brief's old `/project/<slug>/brief` redirects to the mint flow in Earn (below). The legacy `/project/<slug>/<step>` and flat routes redirect to the product the project last opened (`resumeProductOf`, `lib/manual/editor-scope.ts`).
- **Every editor document is per product**: the PCB store (`lib/pcb/store.tsx`, `setDocScope`, `releaseScope`, `usePcbDocScope`) holds no board until a route names a scope, then loads and autosaves `ideeza:pcb:doc:<projectId>:<productId>`; wiring, assembly, the 3D scene, Code files and Preview keep their own scoped keys (`editor-scope.ts`, list in [pcb-architecture.md](../pcb-architecture.md#persisted-state-project-pages-market-wallet-video)). Switching product remounts every editor. A first-row product may read the project's legacy document while it is still the maker's own.
- **A built product's editors open filled from its build** (BUILDLOAD, see [platform-and-projects.md](platform-and-projects.md) › BUILDLOAD): an import banner says what arrived, and a "Bring it in" banner offers the rest, one at a time.
- **Code** (`components/code`): Monaco `dev-editor` + **Blockly** visual editor, AI chat side-panel, code rail/menu, editor switch.
- **3D Module** (`components/3d`): `model-viewer`, **AI generate modal**, sketch mode, three.js canvas, floating tools + left panel + menu bar.
- **Product Preview** (`components/preview`): three.js product assembly — instances, **mate panel**, viewport, toolbar, its own right-click menu.
- **Wiring** (`components/wiring`): wiring canvas + component library + right panel + menu.
- **The mint flow — the Brief, in Earn** (`components/brief`, `lib/brief/mint-flow.ts`; Build and Earn P1-2): the Brief is no longer an editor step but Earn's mint flow at **`/projects/<id>/earn/mint`** (`earnMintHref`), opened from Earn › Overview and Earn › Sell (Start mint · Continue mint · Open the mint summary, from `nextAction`). Four fixed steps, each a page on the app's frame (`brief-card.tsx` `MintStep`): the step's title as the page's one h1 (O02 `PageHeader`; on Done the tick sits over it), then, in the narrow column on the frame's left edge, the one-line *Step N of 4 · Choose · Details · Review and sign · Done* over the shared **`BriefCard`** (a "← Back" link over one surface card), with *Saved as you go* under it; Back walks one step, and from Choose goes to Earn › Sell. Every step has exactly one violet control, its full-width primary CTA (`primaryCta`), aria-disabled with its reason under it while something is missing.
  - **The project is read, never edited here**: the name, description and products come from the engineering record (`ManualProject`, `productsOfProject`) and are edited on the project page — Choose links there (*Edit on the project page*). The old Step 1 words (product name, one-liner, *Also in this project*) and the video step (**Pick your preview**, AI/AR/Skip, the per-product Generate forms, the clip modal and the AR hand-off) are gone from the flow; videos are made in the project's Media tab.
  - **Choose** (`brief/step-choose.tsx`, *Mint your project*): the project read back (name, description, "{k} products"), then **Keep private** (*Free signature · no KYC*) · **Give to the community** (*Wallet needed · no KYC*) · **Sell** (*Wallet + identity check*), stacked at phone width. Continue needs a choice.
  - **Details** (`brief/step-details.tsx`): one form per choice, as the old form step asked. **Sell** is the ONE shared listing form, `ListingFields` in Brief mode (Blockchain and Collection editable, Listing type, **Minting type**, Buy now or Auction terms, Selling percentage, Royalties, Holder benefits, the ownership box), then **License** (a Sell lists at once, and listing needs one: the ladder's L3) and **Share to Innovations** (with *Write your story*, 500 chars). **Give** asks Blockchain Mint · Choose collection · Minting type · **License** (the seven licences, each with an ⓘ) · the ownership confirm · Share to Innovations. **Keep private** asks Blockchain and Collection (*Saved with a free signature — nothing is charged.*), the ownership confirm and Share — no minting type, it is always a lazy signature. **Choose collection** reads the wallet's per-chain rows (`lib/brief/wallet.ts`) with an inline **+ New collection…** (Add is secondary; Escape or Cancel backs out). **Continue to review** is shut on `detailsMissing`: the collection, a Sell's listing problems (shown per field after a blur, at once for a wrong amount, all after a blocked press, with focus on the first), a private mint's ownership confirm, the readiness gate's form rules (ownership, a Give's license), then a Sell's license (*Choose the license it's sold under.*).
  - **Review and sign** (`brief/step-review.tsx`): what is minted (the project as its record holds it, and the terms Details set: shared as, chain, collection, Lazy/Instant, license or the sale's price, share and royalties), then **Videos · n of m ready** — one live row per current product (poster, name, status, a progress bar while rendering). A Sell, a Give and a private mint posted to Innovations run the readiness gate (`gatePurposeOf`, `readinessOf`): a missing video says so and offers **Make videos in Media** (`/projects/<id>?tab=media`), a rendering one never counts (C5). A plain private mint runs no gate (*Videos are optional when you keep it private.*). Then the cost card (`MintCostRows`, Sell and Give), the `WalletFactLine`, a Sell's *Each sale* payouts, and the CTA — `commitCtaLabel`'s **Sign and list · Pay and list · Sign and give · Pay and give** (` · post to Innovations` on a sharing Give) **· Sign and save** — shut on `reviewMissing` (anything Details lacks, with *Change the details*; an Instant mint the wallet can't pay; the gate) and on the gating ladder: Keep private and Give at the mint level (L2), a Sell at the list level (L3), with the steps the flow gates itself left to it (ownership, videos, a Sell's license, and the mint and payout wallet its commit makes), so in practice the demo wallet shows above the CTA with **Connect wallet**. While the wallet request is open it reads **Waiting for your wallet…** and Back is hidden.
  - **The commit** (`brief-app.tsx` `commit`, unchanged) is one wallet request, and its writes run inside the dialog at *confirmed*, in order: the **MintRecord**, then (Sell) the **listing** (`createListing`, source `brief`), `bumpMinted`, then the draft's **`mintedAt`** with the project writes (status Completed, the `brief` step marked, owner confirmed). The listing is built in memory first, so a row it refuses writes nothing; if the listing write fails after the record, the record goes back. A reject or a failure writes nothing and says *Not minted — … Nothing was charged.* under the CTA. Before signing it re-checks that the project isn't already listed and that the readiness gate still passes on what is stored now. **Use lazy mint instead** (an Instant mint the wallet can't pay) switches to Lazy and stays on the review. **Share to Innovations sets `showcasedAt` once**.
  - **Success** (`brief/step-success.tsx`, copy in `lib/brief/success-copy.ts`): **Listed on the marketplace · Given to the community · Saved**, or *Mint complete* while a private mint's videos still render; the ownership proof (`MintProofCard`); for a Sell the **Listing** card (`termsLineOf`) with **View on marketplace**; the ready products' posters; **Go to My Projects**; and Showcase (same readiness gate as the project's Showcase block). **A Brief that minted opens here, read-only** (no step line, no Back, no fields).
  - **The draft** stays `ideeza:brief:draft:<projectId>`. The flow's state is its commerce half (`MintState`, `MINT_STATE_KEYS`: intent, chain, collection, license, the sale's terms, mint type, confirms, story, `mintedAt`); `readMintDraft` migrates any older draft (`normalizeBrief`, so the testnet move and royalties migrations still apply) and every write (`mintDraftJson`) keeps the stored fields the flow doesn't own — the old product words and video fields stay in storage for their readers (the legacy clip migration). The step is stored twice: `step` in the old vocabulary for the status line and Outcome copy (`storedStepOf`: Choose → idea, Details/Review → form), and the flow's own `mintStep`. An old draft opens where it was: idea → Choose, form → Details, a Sell's or Give's preview → Details, a Save's preview → Review, a mint → Success.
  - **Where it opens** (`brief/mint-page.tsx`): after the projects and marketplace stores are read — an unknown id shows the project page's not-found card; a project minted without a Brief (from its page) says *{project} is already minted* with **Open Sell**, never an editable Choose (`useBriefOpens`, decided once per open so a mint made inside the flow never closes it); otherwise the flow. **The old addresses redirect**: `/project/<slug>/brief` (`ProjectWorkspace`) and `/build/<id>/brief` (a build held by a project; a ready unsaved build still goes to `?save=1`, a building one to its chat). A draft a build kept under `build:<id>` hands its chosen intent to the project's draft once (`adoptBuildIntent`) and is removed.
  - **Prompt Help** (`brief/prompt-help-modal.tsx`) — **Help me write it** in the Generate dialog's form (`VideoForm`): *Turn your idea into a video prompt*. It opens on the product's name and description, **Generate a prompt** asks `/api/refine` in `mode: "video"` to rewrite the idea as one 10-second scene, and the **Refined prompt** comes back editable with a copy button that says *Copied* only once the clipboard write resolves. **Use this prompt** writes it into the product's prompt; **Regenerate** re-runs. The model being unreachable is not an error: route and modal fall back to the same deterministic sentence (`lib/brief/video-prompt.ts`). Escape, the × or the overlay closes it and focus returns to the link.
