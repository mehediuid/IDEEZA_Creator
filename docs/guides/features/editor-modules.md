# Features — other editor modules (Code, 3D, Preview, Wiring, Add Brief)

Part of the feature inventory ([index](README.md)). Moved verbatim from `CLAUDE.md` §5 on 2026-09-29. Keep it current: [AGENTS.md › Keeping docs current](../../../AGENTS.md#keeping-docs-current-required).

## Other editor modules
- **The editor opens a product** (`/project/<slug>/products/<productId>/<step>`, step `pcb · code · 3d · assembly · wiring · preview`; `manual/project-workspace.tsx`): the gate resolves the project, refuses a locked (sold-in-full) one, and an unknown product shows "This product isn't in {project}" rather than another product's board. The Brief stays project-level at `/project/<slug>/brief` (no product route). The legacy `/project/<slug>/<step>` and flat routes redirect to the product the project last opened (`resumeProductOf`, `lib/manual/editor-scope.ts`).
- **Every editor document is per product**: the PCB store (`lib/pcb/store.tsx`, `setDocScope`, `releaseScope`, `usePcbDocScope`) holds no board until a route names a scope, then loads and autosaves `ideeza:pcb:doc:<projectId>:<productId>`; wiring, assembly, the 3D scene, Code files and Preview keep their own scoped keys (`editor-scope.ts`, list in [pcb-architecture.md](../pcb-architecture.md#persisted-state-project-pages-market-wallet-video)). Switching product remounts every editor. A first-row product may read the project's legacy document while it is still the maker's own.
- **A built product's editors open filled from its build** (BUILDLOAD, see [platform-and-projects.md](platform-and-projects.md) › BUILDLOAD): an import banner says what arrived, and a "Bring it in" banner offers the rest, one at a time.
- **Code** (`components/code`): Monaco `dev-editor` + **Blockly** visual editor, AI chat side-panel, code rail/menu, editor switch.
- **3D Module** (`components/3d`): `model-viewer`, **AI generate modal**, sketch mode, three.js canvas, floating tools + left panel + menu bar.
- **Product Preview** (`components/preview`): three.js product assembly — instances, **mate panel**, viewport, toolbar, its own right-click menu.
- **Wiring** (`components/wiring`): wiring canvas + component library + right panel + menu.
- **Add Brief** (`components/brief`): a wizard whose steps depend on the intent (`stepsFor(intent)` in `lib/brief/types.ts`, steps named `BriefStepId`, not numbered). **Sell and Give** run idea → preview → form → success, because every product needs its video before either can commit; **Save** runs idea → form → preview → success, since its clip is optional. Every intent gets the preview step; skipping is a **card on the step** (**Add later**), selectable only for a plain Save and locked for Sell, Give and anything with *Share to Innovations* ticked. One `commit()`, called from whichever step is last, opens ONE wallet request and finishes, so the last step's CTA is the commit. The rail lists the live sequence (**Idea · Preview · Ready to sell / Give to community / Save as Private · Done**, answered steps ticked and clickable to go back, locked while the commit runs) above the editor's module rail. A draft stored when steps were 1–4 still opens on the step it reached (`normalizeStep`). Every step renders in the shared **`BriefCard`** frame (surface card, `--radius-2xl`, a "← Back" link above it); the *Saved as you go* note sits under it at reading contrast.
  - **Two homes for one wizard** (`BriefApp({ buildId })`): the project's own Brief at `/project/<slug>/brief`, in the editor chrome (top bar · step rail · module rail), and a saved build's Brief at `/build/<id>/brief`, in the dashboard shell (global sidebar, "← Back" over one card, a one-line step counter, **no module rail and no step rail**). The Brief always has its project before Step 1 renders: a build no project holds is sent to its review with `?save=1`, and one still building goes back to its chat. **Draft scoping:** the draft is `ideeza:brief:draft:<projectId>` in both homes (`briefDraftKey`), hydration and persistence wait for that scope (`hydratedScope`) so a scope change never writes one project's answers over another's. A draft an older build kept under `build:<id>` is adopted once (`adoptBuildDraft`) and removed. A project whose brief already holds work keeps it (`draftHasWork` — a product name only counts while it differs from the project's own); the build's Step 1 answers fill only that brief's blanks and a notice names what travelled and where the rest stayed.
  - **Step 1 shows the project read-only** (`brief/step-1-idea.tsx`): a **Project** row (name · "{k} products", plus "· version n" on a build's brief) with nothing to change — the save step named the project and the project page owns the name. Below it, **Product name**, **One line · what does it do?** (140 chars) and, on a multi-product build, **Also in this project**, each product with an **Edit** that opens its name and description in place (Save writes to the draft, Cancel leaves it). Continue writes the words onto the project's own product rows (`mergeProductEdits`); no row is created or removed. The three intent cards carry their real requirement — **Wallet + identity check** · **Wallet needed · no KYC** · **Free signature · no KYC** — and Continue is disabled, its reason on the page and in `aria-describedby`, until name, one-liner and intent are answered. The heading is **Add a brief** on a build, **What's your idea?** otherwise. Intent cards stack at phone width.
  - **Step 2 makes a real video for every product** (`brief/step-2-video.tsx`, **Pick your preview**): AR (**App coming soon**) · AI (**Recommended**) · Skip (**Available** / **Locked**, with the reason as its title). Under AI, **Videos · n of m ready** lists one disclosure row per current product (poster, name, live status): open, it holds that product's Generate form (`VideoForm`, shared with the Generate dialog — prompt with **Help me write it**, three on-screen lines, **Video quality**), the render's progress with **Cancel render**, or the player with **Regenerate**. The clips are rendered in the browser (`lib/video`, no timer-only jobs) and keep rendering if the maker carries on or leaves. **Quality** is **Low · 360p · Free** or **High · 720p**, tagged *Upgrade* and locked. Continue needs every product's video *started* while a step follows (**Continue to mint setup**); when this step is the commit (a Save), a Save posted to Innovations needs every video *ready*, a plain Save may skip. A rendering video never counts as ready.
  - **Picking AR opens a real hand-off** (`brief/ar-record-panel.tsx`): **Record on your phone**, a **Waiting for phone** chip (a pulsing dot that holds still under `prefers-reduced-motion`), a 200 px QR encoded here by `lib/brief/qr.ts` from `${origin}/brief/ar/<projectId>` (dark-on-light in both themes, so it stays scannable), **HOW IT WORKS**, and **No phone nearby? · Switch to AI instead**. `BriefState.arClip` (`{url, receivedAt} | null`) is written only by the phone app, which isn't released, so Continue stays shut (*The clip hasn't arrived yet.*) and the note says so. An arrived AR clip does not replace the video gate: Sell and Give still need every product's video ready.
  - **The form step** (`brief/step-3-mint.tsx`) — one frame, three forms, over a product card (the headline's poster, or the build's concept image; its play button opens the preview only when a ready video exists; **Videos · n of m ready** goes back to Step 2). Sell **is the marketplace listing form**: `ListingFields` (`projects/listing/listing-fields.tsx`) in Brief mode — Blockchain and Collection editable, **Minting type** as its own field, Buy now or Auction terms, selling percentage, royalties, benefits — then **Share to Innovations** (which reveals *Write your story*, 500 chars). Give asks **Blockchain Mint** · **Choose collection** · **Minting type** · **License** (the seven licences, each with an ⓘ) · the ownership confirm · **Share to Innovations**. Save asks Blockchain and Collection (with *Saved with a free signature — nothing is charged.*), the ownership confirm and Share, with no minting type: a Save is always a lazy signature. **Minting type** (`wallet/mint-type-field.tsx`) is **Lazy** (free now, mints on the first sale) or **Instant** (pays the network fee now); it replaces the old Instant checkbox and gas confirm (`understandGas` is still parsed and ignored). Blockchain is the two testnets; switching chain resets the token and clears the collection. **Choose collection** reads the wallet's per-chain rows (`lib/brief/wallet.ts`) with an inline **+ New collection…** that Escape or Cancel backs out of. A Sell and a Give show the cost card (`MintCostRows`: Lazy pays nothing now; Instant lists the IDZ mint fee, the network fee named for its chain — *test ETH — no real cost* — and **Total to pay now**) and the `WalletFactLine`; a Save shows only the fact line. The full-width CTA is `commitCtaLabel`'s — **Sign and list · Pay and list · Sign and give · Pay and give** (with ` · post to Innovations` on a Give that shares) **· Sign and save** — or **Continue to video ›** when a preview still follows (a Save). It is shut on the first missing reason read top-down (collection · the listing's own problems · ownership · an Instant mint the wallet can't pay · the video gate, with a **See the videos** link), shown as text and `aria-describedby`; while the wallet request is open it reads **Waiting for your wallet…**. The confirms are real checkboxes (Tab and Space reach them). **Royalties** keep what is typed and are validated at once, *Royalties must be between 2 and 10%.*, from `ROYALTY_MIN`/`ROYALTY_MAX`. An auction is checked for an expiry in the past (the clock ticks via `useSyncExternalStore`) and a minimum bid above the buy-now price.
  - **The commit** (`brief-app.tsx` `commit`) is one wallet request, and its writes run inside the dialog at *confirmed*, in order: the **MintRecord**, then (Sell) the **listing**, then the draft's **`mintedAt`** with the project writes (status Completed, the `brief` step marked, owner confirmed). The listing is built in memory first, so a row it refuses writes nothing; if the listing write fails after the record, the record goes back. A reject or a failure writes nothing and says *Not minted — … Nothing was charged.* under the CTA. Before signing it re-checks that the project isn't already listed and that the readiness gate still passes on what is stored now. A project already minted with nothing to list needs no signature. When an Instant mint can't be paid, **Use lazy mint instead** returns to the form with Lazy chosen. **Share to Innovations sets `showcasedAt` once**: a project already showcased keeps the time it was first shown. The body of the commit is `try/finally`, so a throw can't leave the CTA disabled.
  - **The clip modal** (`brief/review-modal.tsx`) — **Auto-Generated Preview**, opened from the form's product card once the headline product has a ready video: the one player (`VideoPlayer`), **Regenerate** and **Done**. **Regenerate goes straight to the videos step** (it needs no confirm — a new take never discards the video in use; `regenerate-confirm.tsx` is gone).
  - **Step 4 says what really happened** (`brief/step-4-success.tsx`, copy in `lib/brief/success-copy.ts`): headings **Listed on the marketplace · Drop is live · Saved**, or *Mint complete* while a Save's videos are still rendering (only a Save can commit with a render running, so only it shows the pending card with each video's progress). Under the subline: the ownership proof (`MintProofCard`); for a Sell, a **Listing** card with the listing's terms as the record holds them now (`termsLineOf`), a note that the price and removal live in the project's Marketplace block, and **View on marketplace** (`/marketplace/<projectId>`); the ready products' posters, each opening its video; **Go to My Projects**; and a Showcase control that runs the same readiness gate as the Outcome block (an already-showcased project opens on its status row with an undo).
  - **Prompt Help** (`brief/prompt-help-modal.tsx`) — **Help me write it** in the Generate form (the Brief's per-product form and the Generate dialog share it): *Turn your idea into a video prompt*. It opens on the product's name and description, **Generate a prompt** asks `/api/refine` in `mode: "video"` to rewrite the idea as one 10-second scene, and the **Refined prompt** comes back editable with a copy button that says *Copied* only once the clipboard write resolves. **Use this prompt** writes it into the product's prompt; **Regenerate** re-runs. The model being unreachable is not an error: route and modal fall back to the same deterministic sentence (`lib/brief/video-prompt.ts`). Escape, the × or the overlay closes it and focus returns to the link.
  - **The rail reads even off-sequence** (`brief/brief-rail.tsx`): when the current step isn't in the running sequence (the regenerate hand-off forces the preview whatever the intent runs), rows are ranked against the canonical `STEP_ORDER`, answered steps tick and the nearest row reads active. The row you are standing on is `aria-disabled`, not `disabled`, so it keeps `aria-current="step"`.
