# Settings

The account's Settings (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-settings-design.md`; Figma User Panel V2 "✅ Settings" `19140:152179`). Settings is about the person; project things stay on the project. Every page has its own URL and every row a deep link (`/settings/<page>#<row>`). Back to [README.md](README.md).

## Shell
- **Frame** (`components/settings/settings-frame.tsx`): the white sheet on the page frame with the settings side-nav, a hairline, and the page — breadcrumb Settings › Group › Page, the page title, then its cards. Below a 768 px page container the side-nav goes and a "Settings" link leads back to the list.
- **Side-nav** (`settings-nav.tsx`): General · Preferences · Audience as accordions (the group holding the current page opens itself), then Plan & Pricing, Policy & Privacy, Activity, Report; the current page is the outlined brand pill. Pages and their order come from `lib/settings/registry.ts`.
- **Cards and rows** (`settings-card.tsx`, `settings-choice.tsx`): a bordered card with its header action (Figma's Edit), rows of label + helper + control, link rows with a chevron, read-only fields ("No information" when empty), the Cancel / Update pair, and the Figma radio-list modal with options that can be disabled with their reason. Arriving at `#<row>` scrolls the row into view and outlines it once.
- **Preferences store** (`lib/settings/prefs.ts`, `use-settings.ts`): one record `ideeza:settings` (v1) for notifications, language, appearance, AI, audience, blocking, privacy and credit alerts; a broken field falls back alone to its default. Data with a home of its own stays there (messages, profile, payment token, theme, PCB settings).
- **Home** `/settings`: Find a setting (every page and row, `lib/settings/search.ts`; Enter opens the first), the four Most visited tiles (IDEEZA Pay, Blocking, AI Properties, Payment), and Looking for something else — the privacy policy and the terms read "Not available yet" because the app has neither page. On a phone the page list follows.
- **Security** `/settings/security`: every Figma row needs accounts, so the page says sign-in isn't in the app yet, lists what will be here, and links to Download your information.
- **Doors**: the account popover's Settings row; ⌘K "Settings", "Plan & pricing", "Wallet & tokens" (opens the wallet dialog) and, while typing, every matching settings row; the Plan & pricing breadcrumb's "Settings".

## KYC Verification
- **Page** `/settings/kyc` (`components/settings/kyc-page.tsx`, Figma `41537:214832`, `41537:215986`): Figma's three levels in one box: the levels on the left as a vertical tab list (↑ ↓ Home End), each with its status badge (Not Submitted · Under Review · Rejected · Approved), and the chosen level on the right (below it on a narrow page). `#intermediate` / `#advanced` open that level.
- **Verify Your Identity (Level 1)** is the demo identity check (`lib/commerce/kyc.ts`, `ideeza:commerce:kyc`): Full Legal Name, Country of Residence and the Marketplace Seller Terms box, all required (errors under each field, focus on the first); Submit for Review approves it at once, labelled Testnet demo. Only `{ status: "verified", at }` is kept; the name and country are never stored. Approved shows the date and **Reset verification**, which removes the key. Email and phone OTP rows wait for sign-in (D18).
- **Intermediate ($1,000) and Advanced ($5,000)** (`kycLevelsOf`): "Not needed yet", or "Needed now" once your sales pass the threshold, with your live total (the full price of every sale of your projects, in dollars at the demo rates) and a progress bar, Figma's What to Expect list, and Start KYC Verification disabled with "Not available yet" (no documents or reviewer in the app, so nothing is limited).
- **The way back**: the Earn ladder's Verify identity opens `/settings/kyc?from=<project page>`; the header then shows **Back to {project}** (`lib/account/return-to.ts` takes only a project path).
- A data note at the foot says the truth: only that you verified, and when, is kept in this browser.

## Get Paid
- **Hub** `/settings/get-paid` (`components/settings/get-paid/hub.tsx`, Figma `41537:182104`): View Withdrawal History; Earning Information › Balance with the balance ›; Tax Information › Tax Residence ›, Tax Identification ›, each showing what is saved.
- **One balance** (D3, `lib/account/payout.ts`): every sale of your projects pays you your share: a share of the project you sold is all yours; a copy sale is split by who held the project when it sold (co-owners added by then and shares sold before it take theirs), after the 2.5 % IDEEZA fee. Test coins count in dollars at fixed demo rates (USDT, USDC, IDZ $1; ETH and WETH $3,750; MATIC $0.50), stated on the page. The balance is that less every withdrawal (a pending one is held).
- **Balance** `/settings/get-paid/balance` (`balance.tsx`, Figma `41537:182534`): Balance Details with Available Balance and **Withdraw Money**; it asks Level 1 KYC and a withdrawal method first, each missing one said under the button with its fix. The tax note ("To withdraw earnings, keep your tax information up to date"). Withdrawal methods with the Default tag and ⋯ Make Default / Remove (Remove Withdrawal Method? names the method that becomes the default; past withdrawals keep their record). Add Withdrawal Method; then the **Get paid in** card (USDT or IDZ, saved on the spot; D14). `?from=<project page>` adds Back to {project}.
- **Add Withdrawal Method** (`add-method-dialog.tsx`, Figma `41537:182189`): Crypto wallet is live; Direct Bank, Payoneer and PayPal are listed as "Not available yet". The crypto step offers the connected demo account first, or any wallet address (`0x` + 40 hex; a receive-only wallet is fine), and an optional name; a duplicate or more than 10 is refused with the reason. Connect closes it and opens the Demo wallet. The first method is the default.
- **Withdraw Money** (`withdraw-dialog.tsx`, Figma `41537:182611`): Available Balance, the method (default first), the whole balance or Other amount (dollars and cents, up to the balance), Withdrawal Fee $0.00, what you receive in your Get paid in token, the total; Withdraw opens the Demo wallet's signing dialog ("Withdraw your earnings", Testnet demo, nothing charged; D18 replaces Figma's OTP). The wallet re-checks the balance before it confirms; the withdrawal is then recorded as complete with a reference (no one processes it in the demo, and no coins move), the balance drops, and the dialog shows Withdraw Request Sent with Go to Withdrawal History.
- **Withdrawal History** `/settings/get-paid/history` (`history.tsx`, Figma `41537:183372`): tiles with the dollars Complete · In Progress · Pending; **Download Statement** (year, and a month or the whole year) downloads a CSV at once with your sales (your share), their IDEEZA fees and your withdrawals, in the coin and in dollars; search (reference, method, amount), status, date range; the table Amount · Date & Time · Transaction ID · Method · Status with each row's own CSV; ten to a page.
- **Tax Residence** (`tax.tsx`, Figma `41537:183180`) and **Tax Identification** (`41537:183204`, `41537:183238`): Save Information stores them in `ideeza:account:payout`; Cancel goes back. A US person adds SSN or EIN (9 digits); only the last four digits are ever kept and the number is never shown again. Nothing is filed.
- **Before Get Paid**: payout requests a project's Earn made (`ideeza:commerce:payouts`) read as pending withdrawals ("Requested in Earn"), so none is lost.
