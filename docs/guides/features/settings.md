# Settings

The account's Settings (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-settings-design.md`; Figma User Panel V2 "✅ Settings" `19140:152179`). Settings is about the person; project things stay on the project. Every page has its own URL and every row a deep link (`/settings/<page>#<row>`). Back to [README.md](README.md).

## Shell
- **Frame** (`components/settings/settings-frame.tsx`): the white sheet on the page frame with the settings side-nav, a hairline, and the page — breadcrumb Settings › Group › Page, the page title, then its cards. Below a 768 px page container the side-nav goes and a "Settings" link leads back to the list.
- **Side-nav** (`settings-nav.tsx`): General · Preferences · Audience as accordions (the group holding the current page opens itself), then Plan & Pricing, Policy & Privacy, Activity, Report; the current page is the outlined brand pill. Pages and their order come from `lib/settings/registry.ts`.
- **Cards and rows** (`settings-card.tsx`, `settings-choice.tsx`): a bordered card with its header action (Figma's Edit), rows of label + helper + control, link rows with a chevron, read-only fields ("No information" when empty), the Cancel / Update pair, and the Figma radio-list modal with options that can be disabled with their reason. Arriving at `#<row>` scrolls the row into view and outlines it once.
- **Preferences store** (`lib/settings/prefs.ts`, `use-settings.ts`): one record `ideeza:settings` (v1) for notifications, language, appearance, AI, audience, blocking, privacy and credit alerts; a broken field falls back alone to its default. Data with a home of its own stays there (messages, profile, payment token, theme, PCB settings).
- **Home** `/settings`: Find a setting (every page and row, `lib/settings/search.ts`; Enter opens the first), the four Most visited tiles (IDEEZA Pay, Blocking, AI Properties, Payment), and Looking for something else — the privacy policy and the terms read "Not available yet" because the app has neither page. On a phone the page list follows.
- **Doors**: the account popover's Settings row; ⌘K "Settings", "Plan & billing", "Pricing", "Wallet & tokens" (opens the wallet dialog) and, while typing, every matching settings row; the Pricing and checkout breadcrumbs' Settings › Plan & billing.

## Plan & billing
`/settings/plan` (`credits/plan-billing-page.tsx`, rows in `lib/settings/rows/plan.ts`): one page in the settings frame, a card per section, each card's id its deep link (`/settings/plan#invoices`). Older `?tab=credits|billing|payment|plans` links (still in the bell) land on the section that replaced the tab and the address becomes its `#section` (`sectionOfTab`). Tab title *Plan & billing · IDEEZA*.
- **Your plan** (`#your-plan`, `credits/your-plan.tsx`): the plan and its price, *Renews on* (or *Ends on*, or *Changes on* with **Keep <plan>** for a scheduled smaller plan), **Auto-renew** (off asks first in Cancel subscription, which offers Tinkerer instead; on again keeps the plan), the month's AI credits and when they were added, Projects and Contributors as meters against the plan's amounts, Exports. **Change plan** opens Pricing.
- **Credits** (`#credits`, `credits/credits-summary.tsx`): the balance, the credits you have as lots (added, expiry, left), and *Where they went* by project, this month or all time (`lib/account/credit-spend.ts`: a build on the project it became, a drawing on its chat's project, a chat not yet a project linking to the chat; refunds cost nothing; a month's total is the monthly limit's). **Credit history** opens every lot, spent and expired ones too.
- **Buy credits** (`#buy-credits`, `credits/buy-credits-card.tsx`): Starter, Pro (most popular), Power as rows with bonus and price (struck through under a plan discount), and a custom amount (at least 50 at $0.10) with *Better deal available* when a pack gives as many for less; each Buy opens the checkout. **Compare discounts** shows the plans' credit discounts and links to Pricing. The account popover's and History's Buy credits land here.
- **Credit alerts and limits** (`#credit-alerts`, `credits/credit-alerts.tsx`, rules in `lib/create/credit-limits.ts`, values in `ideeza:settings` › credits): read as rows, Edit → form → Update.
  - *Running low at* (8): the account popover warns at or under it; the row says how many one-product builds that still covers (`lowMarkWhy`), so the warning comes before a build has to wait.
  - *Remind me before credits expire* (14 days): the row shows the next lot to expire.
  - *Monthly spending limit* (none): a build whose turn would cross it pauses (*Change your limit →* links here) and a drawing is refused, nothing charged; the row says what is spent and left.
  - *Ask before a purchase over* (never): checkout and Buy credits ask once more above it.
  - *Auto top-up* (off, and says so): a build paused for credits asks the Demo wallet to sign for the chosen pack, once per pause.
- **Pay with** (`#pay-with`, `credits/pay-with.tsx`): the Demo wallet once connected, the default, with the token it pays in (⋮ Manage wallet · Pay with IDZ · Pay with USDT); **Add payment method** lists Card and PayPal as not available yet. Get paid's *Get paid in* is the earning side.
- **Billing details** (`#billing-details`, `credits/billing-details.tsx`, `ideeza:account:billing`): optional name or company, tax ID, address and email that every invoice prints under *Billed to* (with no name set, your legal name from Settings › Account, else your profile name).
- **Invoices** (`#invoices`, `credits/billing-history.tsx`): every payment for a plan or credits, searchable by order or invoice number and by year, ten to a page, each opening its invoice (`/settings/plan/invoice/[orderId]`, *← Invoices* back here).

## Pricing

- **Choose a plan**: Monthly | Annual (save 20%), Tinkerer · Builder (most popular) · Disruptor cards and a wide Free card, each button by how the plan stands to yours (Current plan · Upgrade → checkout · Switch to annual · Downgrade or Switch to Free → a dialog, from the period's end · Starts on).

Rows the app doesn't do yet are kept and marked **Coming later**: AI queue, Help & support, Public and private components, Video with IDEEZA AR, Hire a freelancer or a manufacturer, APK generator. The cards mark the queue the same way, and a smaller plan's losses no longer name a queue.
- **Pricing FAQs**: General and one tab per plan, *Was this helpful?* kept per browser.
- The doors in: Your plan's Change plan, the sidebar's *Upgrade to …*, History's plan strip *See plans*, the video dialog's *See plans*, Compare discounts, ⌘K *Pricing*, and the checkout's *See plans*. The checkout's breadcrumb is Settings › Plan & billing › Pricing › Payment details for a plan, Settings › Plan & billing › Payment details for credits.

## Privacy & safety
- `/settings/privacy` (`components/settings/privacy-page.tsx`, rows `lib/settings/rows/privacy.ts`, rules `lib/settings/audience.ts`): Phase 1's Profile locking, Profile & Share, Blocking and Report in one page; their old addresses redirect to its rows. Every row saves at once. The five "who can" rows nothing reads yet (follow, see who you follow, add you to a team, invite you to a team conversation, save and link; V4) have no UI; their fields stay in `ideeza:settings`.
- **Your profile**:
  - **View as public** → `/creators/me?view=public`.
  - **Lock your profile** (`audience.locked`, rule `seesLockedProfile`, which the profile reads): people who don't work with you on a project see only your name, photo and "This profile is locked"; your project teams see all of it. The row says what stays public: projects you shared on Innovations, your marketplace listings and your comments, and messages under your message rule.
  - **Show my location and local time** (`audience.showLocalTime`, off): others see your city and local time under your name; off, only you see your city. With no city in your profile the row says to add one from your profile.
  - **Search engines** (`privacy.searchEngines`, off): while off, your profile page carries `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` (`profile-robots.tsx`).
- **Who can reach you** (value buttons that open the radio dialog, `audience-controls.tsx`):
  - **Who can message you** (D13, Messages' own store `allowNew`; the Messages gear opens `#message`): Anyone · People you work with or who own a copy or hold a share · No one new. A refused start in `/messages/new` says why.
  - **Who can comment on your shared projects** (`audience.comment`): Anyone · People you work with (your project teams) · Only you. On your own post, someone else's comment whose writer this leaves out right now is held: you read "Held: its writer can't comment under your rule right now", other visitors read "Hidden by the maker", its writer still reads it, and every count leaves it out. It comes back when the rule lets its writer in. Your post says "Who can comment: …" with Change.
  - **Take a break** (`audience.quietUntil`): Off · 24 hours · 7 days, "On until …" in your date format. While on, new message requests from people who aren't known to you are refused (listing questions, purchases, your teams, share holders, copy owners and people you wrote to still reach you), Requests says "You're taking a break until …", and on your shared projects only people you work with can comment (the rest are held). It ends by itself.
- **Blocked people** (`blocked-people.tsx`): one list, newest first, each with when and the conversations you have with them ("Conversation about …"), and **Unblock**. **Block someone** searches the people you've met (Messages, comment threads on your posts or that you wrote in, people who bought from you; `peopleMet`). One blocked list, Messages' own, which a profile's Block and a conversation's Block write too. A block means: no messages either way; their comments read "From someone you blocked" to you on every post and are held off your shared projects; they aren't offered to @mention; and on a post whose maker you blocked (or who blocked you) a line replaces the composer and no comment offers Reply.
- **Hidden words** (`hidden-words.tsx`, `blocking.hiddenWords`): chips with the input after them (comma or Enter adds, Backspace removes the last), "Remaining n of 500", Cancel / Save once changed. A plain word matches the whole word in any case and its #tag; "#word" only the tag. Text and words are compared in NFC with zero-width characters taken out (`matchForm`), so Bangla typed with a ZWJ or ZWNJ, or a vowel sign typed as two code points, still matches. They hide someone else's comment on your shared projects ("Hidden: it uses a word you hide" to you) and a message request (out of Requests and its count, with Show).
- **Your reports** (`your-reports.tsx`): every report this browser sent (profiles, posts, comments, messages, Report a problem; `lib/data/reports.ts`), newest first, each opening to the words, reason, your note, reference and status ("Received, not reviewed yet"; a problem report "Sent to the IDEEZA team"). A line says what to do in danger: contact local emergency services, IDEEZA can't act in real time. "Reports about your work" waits until reviews exist.
## Notifications
- **Page** `/settings/notifications` (`components/settings/notifications-page.tsx`, V5): every control saves as it changes into `ideeza:settings` › `notifications` (and `appearance.keepNotices`). The bell lists every notice; the page decides only what pops up and what adds to the bell's count. Rows are `lib/settings/rows/notifications.ts`.
  - **Pop-ups**: **Show pop-ups** Right away · Once a day · Paused (`digest` + `popupsPaused`; an older weekly summary reads as once a day). Right away: builds and Blog reviews pop up on the page, and outside the tab as below. Once a day: nothing pops up as it happens; the first look after the day turns, one toast sums up what came in since the last one (`ideeza:notifications:summary`), and the bell groups by day. Paused: nothing pops up; the bell still lists and counts.
  - **Quiet hours**: on/off; when on, **Between** two times (this device's time, across midnight) and **Let sales through** (off by default). Inside the window nothing pops up and the bell's count doesn't grow; a sale does when let through.
  - **Keep notices until I close them**: the Blog toast and the daily summary stay until closed, and a browser pop-up stays on screen (`requireInteraction`); greyed while pop-ups are paused.
  - **Pop-ups outside this tab**: the browser's permission, live — Allow (asks the browser), Allowed, Blocked (how to unblock in site settings), or not supported. IDEEZA also asks once, ever, when you start a build (the build gate's Generate), and only while builds can pop up (`ideeza:notifications:asked`).
  - **What you hear about**: Sales and bids (with **Bids to hear about**: every bid or $10–$1,000 or more, greyed while sales are off), Builds and videos, Credits and plan (with a link to the low mark), Messages and requests, Comments and replies, Team conversations, Blog reviews, each with its own switch (`channels[kind].web`). Switched off, a kind still lists in the bell but doesn't pop up or count. Then one line: email and phone notifications come with sign-in. Follow, Policy and Other stay in the record but aren't shown (nothing sends them).
  - **Muted projects**: each with Unmute; empty, it says muting happens from a notice's ⋯ in the bell.
- **The notice model** (`lib/notifications/`): `sources.ts` derives notices from the stores the app keeps — builds (ready, needs a retry, paused for credits → Plan & pricing › Credits; a build with `blocked: "cap"` reads "Paused — monthly limit reached" → Credits `#credit-alerts`), Blog results, Messages (a new message in the Inbox, a new Request; read when opened there), project team conversations, the marketplace (a sale of your listing or copy, urgent; a bid valued at Get paid's demo rates, `lib/account/payout.ts`, shown as "0.5 ETH (about $1,875 at the demo rate)"; your auction ending within an hour), credits and the plan (at or under the low mark, bought credits expiring within the reminder days, the plan renewing, changing or ending within three days), a ready video take, and comments by others on projects you shared. `filter.ts` decides what is heard (kind switch, muted project, bid threshold, hidden words), the count and the pop-up gate; `popups.ts` the daily summary and browser pop-ups; `read.ts` + `read-store.ts` keep what the bell marked read in `ideeza:notifications:read`, beside the Blog's own seen state.
- **The bell** (`components/notifications/`): one list for the sidebar's bell, the phone's and the editors' top bar; it never leaves a notice out for a setting. Unread notices carry a dot; the count is unread notices that are heard, less messages and team conversations (those count on the Messages badge only) and less what came in during quiet hours. A notice that uses one of your hidden words (Privacy & safety) shows "Hidden: it uses a word you hide" and isn't heard. Read ones stay 30 days. The header's ⋯ has **Mark all as read** and **Notification settings**; a notice about a project has its own ⋯ **Mute this project**, with Undo in the panel.
- **Pop-ups**: the build attention toast and the Blog result toast follow the pop-up mode, quiet hours, the kind's switch, muted projects and hidden words. `notice-popups.tsx` (mounted at the root, editors included) shows a heard notice as the browser's own pop-up when the tab is hidden and the browser allows it (a build finishing on the chat open in that hidden tab included; tagged by notice, so two tabs show it once; a click opens it), and the once-a-day summary toast.

## Account
- **Page** `/settings/account` (`components/settings/account-page.tsx`, rows `lib/settings/rows/account.ts`): five cards, each one's id its search row. The Phase 1 Profile, Personal Information and Security pages are gone; their routes redirect here (Security to `#sign-in`).
- **Public profile** (`#public-profile`): your photo, name and headline as others see them, and **Edit profile**, which opens your profile with the Edit profile dialog open (`/creators/<handle>?edit=1`). Edit profile is the only editor of the public profile; Settings holds none of its fields.
- **Handle** (`#handle`): your profile's address as a link, **Copy** (the full address; the button reads "Copied"), and **Edit**: 3–30 lower-case letters, numbers or hyphens, checked as you type against the sample creators' handles, with the address it gives; empty keeps `/creators/me`; changing it says links to the old address stop working. Says the address opens your profile in this browser only until sign-in arrives.
- **Legal name** (`#legal-name`): private, one line (80), Edit → Save. Stored alone in `ideeza:account:identity` v2 (`lib/account/identity.ts`); a Phase 1 record's first and last name become it, and its gender, permanent address and shipping addresses are dropped on read. An invoice prints it under *Billed to* when Billing details has no name. For the identity check to use the same value, `useIdentity` reads it and `saveLegalName` writes it.
- **Wallet** (`#wallet`): the connected demo wallet's short address, its account and network, and the Testnet demo badge, else "Not connected"; **Manage** / **Connect wallet** opens the Demo wallet dialog.
- **Sign-in and security** (`#sign-in`): one line that sign-in isn't in the app yet (password, 2-step sign-in, devices and deleting the account come with it), that your work lives in this browser, and **Download your information** → `/settings/data#download`.
- **Shipping address**: not kept in Settings. No checkout ships a physical copy to you yet (a physical copy is bought only by a demo creator, through the wallet dialog), so nothing asks for one.

## Personal Data
- `/settings/personal-data` (Figma `22048:35199`, `personal-data-page.tsx`): the **Download Your Information** row opens the Figma wizard in one dialog (`download-wizard.tsx`): Available Information (everything) or Specific Information → Choose Information (nine kinds, each with what this browser holds of it) → Choose method to save your data (Download to device) → Create Files to download with **Date Range** (All Time · Last Year · Last 6/3 Months · Last Month · Last Week · Custom with two dates), **Format** (HTML · JSON) and **Media Quality** (High: photos and files at full size and your videos · Medium: photos made smaller, no videos · Low: no media), each its own radio modal. Back on every step after the first; "Step n of m".
- **Create File to download** builds a `.zip` in the browser and downloads it (`ideeza-information-<date>.zip`), with progress while it reads photos and files; the page then says the name, size and file count. Nothing is sent anywhere and no store is written.
- **What's in it** (`lib/data/export.ts`, `export-html.ts`, `export-browser.ts`, `zip.ts`): every `ideeza…` key in one of Product · Marketplace · Coin (credits and payments) · Newsfeed (Innovations) · Blog · Message · Activity · Profile Information, plus **Project files** — each product's board as a real `.kicad_pcb` (the PCB exporter) beside the editor document, firmware files as files, wiring, 3D, assembly and preview as JSON. Interface state and the editors' backups stay out; a key nobody has named goes with Profile. Of a post's comments only yours and the ids you reported leave. A date range keeps each record's dated lines and drops an older project's keys and files with it; undated things (profile, settings) stay whole. HTML gives a readable `index.html` per kind and one at the root; JSON one file per record and a `manifest.json`. Photos, attachments, project media and clips come from IndexedDB through each store's own reader.
- The zip is "stored" (no compression) with CRC-32 and UTF-8 names, written without a dependency; it opens in Finder, Windows and `unzip`.
- Not in Figma's list: its second "Marketplace Information", "Send to Manufacturer" (no such data) and "Security & Login" (no accounts). "Notify" waits for sign-in; the file is ready at once, so there is no 72-hour window.
- A card under the row, **What your file holds**, lists the nine kinds with a line of what this browser holds of each.

## Your activity
- `/settings/activity` (Figma `22048:35243`, `activity-page.tsx`): the card **Actions you have done in IDEEZA** with four rows and their counts, each opening its list at `?list=appreciated|comments|videos|searches`. "Logged in history" needs sign-in and isn't shown.
- **Appreciated** (D16): posts you appreciated (`ideeza:innovations:reactions`), newest first and said to be without days, because an appreciation keeps no date. **Comments**: every comment you wrote across the posts' stored threads, by day, each linking to its comment.
- **Videos you have watched**: a post's video counts once it has played 3 s (`watch-recorder.tsx` round the post's video stage; skipping ahead doesn't count; the same video again that day moves up). **Your search history**: what you search on Innovations and Explore marketplace (typing one query is one line) and the ⌘K queries you open a result for, with where and what you opened; Innovations and Explore lines run the search again.
- Both logs live in `ideeza:activity` (`lib/data/activity-log.ts`, v1): 90 days, at most 200 lines each, newest first, grouped Today · Yesterday · date. **Clear watch history** and **Clear search history** are confirmed.

## KYC Verification
- **Page** `/settings/kyc` (`components/settings/kyc-page.tsx`, Figma `41537:214832`, `41537:215986`): Figma's three levels in one box: the levels on the left as a vertical tab list (↑ ↓ Home End), each with its status badge (Not Submitted · Under Review · Rejected · Approved), and the chosen level on the right (below it on a narrow page). `#intermediate` / `#advanced` open that level.
- **Verify Your Identity (Level 1)** is the demo identity check (`lib/commerce/kyc.ts`, `ideeza:commerce:kyc`): Full Legal Name, Country of Residence and the Marketplace Seller Terms box, all required (errors under each field, focus on the first); Submit for Review approves it at once, labelled Testnet demo. Email and phone OTP rows wait for sign-in (D18).
- **Intermediate ($1,000) and Advanced ($5,000)** (`kycLevelsOf`): "Not needed yet", or "Needed now" once your sales pass the threshold, with your live total (the full price of every sale of your projects, in dollars at the demo rates) and a progress bar, Figma's What to Expect list, and Start KYC Verification disabled with "Not available yet" (no documents or reviewer in the app, so nothing is limited).
- **The way back**: the Earn ladder's Verify identity opens `/settings/kyc?from=<project page>`; the header then shows **Back to {project}** (`lib/account/return-to.ts` takes only a project path).
- A data note at the foot says the truth: only that you verified, and when, is kept in this browser.

## Get Paid
- **Hub** `/settings/get-paid` (`components/settings/get-paid/hub.tsx`, Figma `41537:182104`): View Withdrawal History; Earning Information › Balance with the balance ›; Tax Information › Tax Residence ›, Tax Identification ›, each showing what is saved.
- **One balance** (D3, `lib/account/payout.ts`): every sale of your projects pays you your share: a share of the project you sold is all yours; a copy sale is split by who held the project when it sold (co-owners added by then and shares sold before it take theirs), after the 2.5 % IDEEZA fee. Test coins count in dollars at fixed demo rates (USDT, USDC, IDZ $1; ETH and WETH $3,750; MATIC $0.50), stated on the page. The balance is that less every withdrawal (a pending one is held).
- **Balance** `/settings/get-paid/balance` (`balance.tsx`, Figma `41537:182534`): Balance Details with Available Balance and **Withdraw Money**; it asks Level 1 KYC and a withdrawal method first, each missing one said under the button with its fix. The tax note ("To withdraw earnings, keep your tax information up to date"). Withdrawal methods with the Default tag and ⋯ Make Default / Remove (Remove Withdrawal Method? names the method that becomes the default; past withdrawals keep their record). Add Withdrawal Method; then the **Get paid in** card (USDT or IDZ, saved on the spot; D14). `?from=<project page>` adds Back to {project}.
- **Add Withdrawal Method** (`add-method-dialog.tsx`, Figma `41537:182189`): Crypto wallet is live; Direct Bank, Payoneer and PayPal are listed as "Not available yet". The crypto step offers the connected demo account first, or any wallet address (`0x` + 40 hex; a receive-only wallet is fine), and an optional name; a duplicate or more than 10 is refused with the reason. Connect closes it and opens the Demo wallet. The first method is the default.
- **Withdraw Money** (`withdraw-dialog.tsx`, Figma `41537:182611`): Available Balance, the method (default first), the whole balance or Other amount (dollars and cents, up to the balance), Withdrawal Fee $0.00, what you receive in your Get paid in token, the total; Withdraw opens the Demo wallet's signing dialog ("Withdraw your earnings", Testnet demo, nothing charged; D18 replaces Figma's OTP). The wallet re-checks the balance before it confirms; the withdrawal is then recorded as complete with a reference (no one processes it in the demo, and no coins move), the balance drops, and the dialog shows Withdraw Request Sent with Go to Withdrawal History.
- **Withdrawal History** `/settings/get-paid/history` (`history.tsx`, Figma `41537:183372`): tiles with the dollars Complete · In Progress · Pending; **Download Statement** (year, and a month or the whole year) downloads a CSV at once with your sales (your share), their IDEEZA fees and your withdrawals, in the coin and in dollars; search (reference, method, amount), status, date range; the table Amount · Date & Time · Transaction ID · Method · Status with each row's own CSV; ten to a page.
- **Tax Residence** (`tax.tsx`, Figma `41537:183180`) and **Tax Identification** (`41537:183204`, `41537:183238`): Save Information stores them in `ideeza:account:payout`; Cancel goes back. A US person adds SSN or EIN (9 digits); only the last four digits are ever kept and the number is never shown again. Nothing is filed.
- **Before Get Paid**: payout requests a project's Earn made (`ideeza:commerce:payouts`) read as pending withdrawals ("Requested in Earn"), so none is lost.

## Appearance & accessibility
`/settings/appearance` (new page; `components/settings/appearance-page.tsx`). Every control saves as it changes.
- **Theme**: stays in the account menu (D11); the row explains that and **Open account menu** opens the sidebar's account popover (in the phone drawer on a phone) with focus on its first row (`openAccountMenu()` in `dashboard/sidebar-account.tsx`).
- **Text size** Default · Large, **High contrast**, **Reduce motion** Match system · Reduce: `<html data-text-size="large" | data-contrast="high" | data-motion="reduce">` (`lib/settings/appearance.ts`). A pre-paint script in `<head>` sets them before the first paint; `AppearanceSync` (`components/settings/appearance-sync.tsx`, root layout) follows changes. Large steps each `--font-size-*` and its `--line-height-*` up one DS step (2xs–4xl; the display sizes have no larger line height to pair with, so they stay); High contrast gives secondary and tertiary text the primary text token and every border the strong border token, in both themes (`tokens.css`); Reduce joins the reduced-motion rule in `reset.css`, and scripted motion asks `motionReduced()` (smooth scrolling, the assembly viewer).
- **Single-key shortcuts** (on by default): off, the PCB (H, V, R), 3D (G, R, S, H, L, B, Y, 0) and Connection Map (V, L) editors ignore a key that types a character on its own; ⌘/Ctrl/Alt shortcuts and Escape, Delete, Space, Enter keep working (`lib/settings/keys.ts` `singleKeyShortcutsAllowed`, first line of each editor's keydown handler). Code, Wiring and Preview have no single-key shortcuts.
- **Units, grid and hotkeys**: a link to the active project's PCB editor, where the Setting menu holds them (D12); greyed with the reason when there is no project.
- **Keep notices until I close them**: the choice is stored (`appearance.keepNotices`).
- **Data saver**: a post's video doesn't preload (not a byte until Play); videos never autoplay anyway.

## AI Properties
`/settings/ai` (the home's AI Properties tile; `components/settings/ai-page.tsx`, its route reads this deployment's keys at request time).
- **Where your AI requests go**: Text → Pollinations' keyless text model; Images → AI Horde's volunteer GPU pool (or Pollinations when the deployment has `POLLINATIONS_TOKEN`); 3D models → Meshy when `MESHY_API_KEY` is set, else a sample model.
- **Use the volunteer GPU pool for images** (on by default): off, concept images and the 3D editor's AI Generate send `pool: "off"`; the routes never send them to the Horde and, with no other image service, refuse with `pool-off` (409) and say so on the concept card and in the 3D dialog. Disabled with its reason when the deployment draws images with Pollinations.
- **AI help in the editors** (on by default): off hides every editor's AI assistant (the robot button and its chat in the PCB, 3D and Preview left panels; the Code Development activity-bar button and the Blockly AI tab) and the 3D toolbar's AI Generate.
