# Settings

The account's Settings, v2 (owner decisions V1–V12, 2026-10-20; Phase 1 spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-settings-design.md`, Figma User Panel V2 "✅ Settings" `19140:152179`). Settings is about the person, and holds only what works today; project things stay on the project, and a value edited elsewhere (Edit profile, the PCB editor) is linked, never copied. Every page has its own URL and every row a deep link (`/settings/<page>#<row>`). Back to [README.md](README.md).

## Shell
- **Frame** (`components/settings/settings-frame.tsx`): the white sheet on the page frame with the settings side-nav, a hairline, and the page — breadcrumb Settings › Page, the page title, then its cards. Below a 768 px page container the side-nav goes and a "Settings" link leads back to the list.
- **Side-nav** (`settings-nav.tsx`, V3): one flat list drawn like the app sidebar (32 px rows, 14 px text, 16 px icons, the current page on the raised surface): Account · Notifications · Privacy & safety · Language & region · Appearance & accessibility · AI & your data · Plan & billing · Get paid; each label is its page title. Pages come from `lib/settings/registry.ts`; Phase 1 pages are legacy ids whose routes call `redirectLegacy()` (`lib/settings/legacy-route.ts`), keeping the query and landing on the row's `#`.
- **Cards and rows** (`settings-card.tsx`, `settings-choice.tsx`): a bordered card with its header action (Figma's Edit), rows of label + helper + control, link rows with a chevron, read-only fields ("No information" when empty), the Cancel / Update pair, and the Figma radio-list modal with options that can be disabled with their reason. Arriving at `#<row>` scrolls the row into view and outlines it once.
- **Preferences store** (`lib/settings/prefs.ts`, `use-settings.ts`): one record `ideeza:settings` (v1) for notifications, language, appearance, AI, audience, blocking, privacy and credit alerts; a broken field falls back alone to its default. Data with a home of its own stays there (messages, profile, payment token, theme, PCB settings).
- **Find a setting** (`lib/settings/search.ts`, the home and ⌘K): filler words dropped ("how do I turn off emails"), a word may start a word of the row, plurals and -ing/-ed fold, everyday words also look for ours (dark → theme, delete → erase, kyc → identity; worth half a typed match), rows holding the most words win, then label > keywords > help. A row reached only through its page's name adds nothing beyond the page hit. Nav pages only.
- **Home** `/settings` (`settings-home.tsx`): Find a setting; on a phone the page list right after it; then status cards, each leading to the page that changes it: **This browser** (kept only here, last backup or Never, whether the browser promised to keep the data; Download a backup, Restore), **Plan and credits** (plan, renewal, credits left with Running low; Buy credits), **Privacy at a glance** (profile open or locked, who can message you, location shown, a break until when), and **Get paid** only once something of yours is listed or sold (available, withdrawn, methods; Withdraw). The Phase 1 Most visited tiles and the two legal cards are gone (nothing counted visits; the legal pages don't exist yet).
- **Doors**: the account popover's Settings row; ⌘K "Settings", "Plan & pricing", "Wallet & tokens" (opens the wallet dialog) and, while typing, every matching settings row; the Plan & pricing breadcrumb's "Settings".

## Plan & pricing
Plan & pricing keeps its own dedicated page, frame and four tabs (owner, 2026-10-21: the Settings v2 rebuild into the settings frame was undone); the Settings side-nav's *Plan & pricing* opens it (`components/credits/`, see [ai-create-flow.md](ai-create-flow.md)); these are the settings added inside it. Deep links name the tab: `/settings/plan?tab=credits#credit-alerts`, `?tab=billing#billing-details` (rows in `lib/settings/rows/plan.ts`, one per setting).
- **Credit alerts and limits** (Credits tab, `credits/credit-alerts.tsx`, rules in `lib/create/credit-limits.ts`, values in `ideeza:settings` › credits): one card read as rows, Edit → form → Update.
  - *Running low at* (8 credits, two one-product builds): the account popover says *Running low on credits*, makes Buy credits primary and turns its ring to the warning ink at or under this balance.
  - *Remind me before credits expire* (14 days): the row shows the next lot to expire (its credits and date); the reminder notice itself belongs to Notifications.
  - *Monthly spending limit* (none): credits spent in the calendar month, refunds put back. A build whose turn comes and would cross it is paused (*Paused — monthly limit*, *You reached your monthly limit*, **Change your limit →** here; the toast offers **Change limit**) and starts by itself once the limit is raised or the month turns; a concept drawing that would cross it is refused on its card with the same reason and link, nothing charged. The row says what is spent and what is left.
  - *Ask before a purchase over* (never): above this many dollars, checkout and Buy credits ask *Confirm this purchase* once before the wallet opens.
  - *Auto top-up* (off, and the row says so): when a build pauses for credits, the Demo wallet asks you to sign for the chosen pack (Starter · Pro · Power, at your plan's discount), once per pause (tried pauses are kept in `ideeza:credits:auto-top-up`), only when the build would then start within the monthly limit; the build resumes the way it does after Buy credits. The payment reads *IDZ · demo wallet · auto top-up* in Billing history and on its invoice.
- **Billing details** (Billing history tab, `credits/billing-details.tsx`, `lib/account/billing-details.ts`, `ideeza:account:billing` v1): Name or company, Tax ID (VAT / GST / EIN), Address, Billing email — Edit → form → Update, an email that isn't one is refused with how to fix it. Every invoice prints them under *Billed to* (with no name set, your legal name from Settings › Account, else your profile name).
- **Payment method**: the token wording is *Pay with* (row and menu: Pay with IDZ / Pay with USDT); Get Paid's *Get paid in* is the earning side.
- **Downgrade and Switch to Free**: both dialogs show what the smaller plan allows against what you have (projects; the most contributors on one project) with the plan-details meters, and say that nothing is removed.

## Privacy & safety
- `/settings/privacy` (`components/settings/privacy-page.tsx`, rows `lib/settings/rows/privacy.ts`, rules `lib/settings/audience.ts`): Phase 1's Profile locking, Profile & Share, Blocking and Report in one page; their old addresses redirect to its rows. Every row saves at once. The five "who can" rows nothing reads yet (follow, see who you follow, add you to a team, invite you to a team conversation, save and link; V4) have no UI; their fields stay in `ideeza:settings`.
- **Your profile**:
  - **View as public** → `/creators/me?view=public`.
  - **Lock your profile** (`audience.locked`, rule `seesLockedProfile`, which the profile reads): people who don't work with you on a project see only your name, photo and "This profile is locked"; your project teams see all of it. The row says what stays public: projects you shared on Innovations, your marketplace listings and your comments, and messages under your message rule.
  - **Show my location and local time** (`audience.showLocalTime`, off): others see your city and local time under your name; off, only you see your city. With no city in your profile the row says to add one from your profile.
  - **Search engines** (`privacy.searchEngines`, off): while off, your profile page carries `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` (`profile-robots.tsx`).
- **Who can reach you** (value buttons that open the radio dialog, `audience-controls.tsx`):
  - **Who can message you** (D13, Messages' own store `allowNew`; the Messages gear opens `#message`): Anyone · People you work with or who own a copy or hold a share · No one new. A refused start in `/messages/new` says why.
  - **Who can comment on your shared projects** (`audience.comment`): Anyone · People you work with (your project teams) · Only you. On your own post, someone else's comment whose writer this leaves out right now is held: you read "Held: its writer can't comment under your rule right now", other visitors read "Hidden by the maker", its writer still reads it, and every count leaves it out. It comes back when the rule lets its writer in. Your post says "Who can comment: …" with Change.
  - **Take a break** (`audience.quietUntil`): Off · 24 hours · 7 days, "On until …" in your date format. While on, new message requests from people who aren't known to you are refused (listing questions, purchases, your teams, share holders, copy owners and people you wrote to still reach you), Requests says "You're taking a break until …", and on your shared projects only people you work with can comment (the rest are held). It ends by itself.
- **Blocked people** (`blocked-people.tsx`): one list, newest first, each with when and the conversations you have with them ("Conversation about …"), and **Unblock**. **Block someone** searches the people you've met (Messages, comment threads on your posts or that you wrote in, people who bought from you; `peopleMet`). One blocked list, Messages' own, which a profile's Block and a conversation's Block write too. A block means: no messages either way; their comments read "From someone you blocked" to you on every post and are held off your shared projects; they aren't offered to @mention; and on a post whose maker you blocked (or who blocked you) a line replaces the composer and no comment offers Reply.
- **Hidden words** (`hidden-words.tsx`, `blocking.hiddenWords`): chips with the input after them (comma or Enter adds, Backspace removes the last), "Remaining n of 500", Cancel / Save once changed. A plain word matches the whole word in any case and its #tag; "#word" only the tag. Text and words are compared in NFC with zero-width characters taken out (`matchForm`), so Bangla typed with a ZWJ or ZWNJ, or a vowel sign typed as two code points, still matches. They hide someone else's comment on your shared projects ("Hidden: it uses a word you hide" to you) and a message request (out of Requests and its count, with Show).
- **Your reports** (`your-reports.tsx`): every report this browser sent (profiles, posts, comments, messages, Report a problem; `lib/data/reports.ts`), newest first, each opening to the words, reason, your note, reference and status ("Received, not reviewed yet"; a problem report "Sent to the IDEEZA team"). A line says what to do in danger: contact local emergency services, IDEEZA can't act in real time. "Reports about your work" waits until reviews exist.
## Notifications
- **Page** `/settings/notifications` (`components/settings/notifications-page.tsx`, V5): every control saves as it changes into `ideeza:settings` › `notifications` (and `appearance.keepNotices`). The bell lists every notice; the page decides only what pops up and what adds to the bell's count. Rows are `lib/settings/rows/notifications.ts`.
  - **Pop-ups**: **Show pop-ups** Right away · Once a day · Paused (`digest` + `popupsPaused`; an older weekly summary reads as once a day). Right away: builds and Blog reviews pop up on the page, and outside the tab as below. Once a day: nothing pops up as it happens; the first look after the day turns, one toast sums up what came in since the last one (`ideeza:notifications:summary`), and the bell groups by day. Paused: nothing pops up; the bell still lists and counts.
  - **Quiet hours**: on/off; when on, **Between** two times (this device's time, across midnight) and **Let sales through** (off by default). Inside the window nothing pops up and the bell's count doesn't grow; a sale does when let through.
  - **Keep notices until I close them**: the Blog toast and the daily summary stay until closed, and a browser pop-up stays on screen (`requireInteraction`); greyed while pop-ups are paused.
  - **Pop-ups outside this tab**: the browser's permission, live — Allow (asks the browser), Allowed, Blocked (how to unblock in site settings), or not supported. IDEEZA also asks once, ever, when you start a build (the build gate's Generate), and only while builds can pop up (`ideeza:notifications:asked`).
  - **What you hear about**: Sales and bids (with **Bids to hear about**: every bid or $10–$1,000 or more, greyed while sales are off), Builds and videos, Credits and plan (with a link to the low mark), Messages and requests, Comments and replies, Team conversations, Blog reviews, each with its own switch (`channels[kind].web`). Switched off, a kind still lists in the bell but doesn't pop up or count. Then one line: email and phone notifications come with sign-in. Follow, Policy and Other stay in the record but aren't shown (nothing sends them).
  - **Muted projects**: each with Unmute; empty, it says muting happens from a notice's ⋯ in the bell.
- **The notice model** (`lib/notifications/`): `sources.ts` derives notices from the stores the app keeps — builds (ready, needs a retry, paused for credits → Plan & billing › Credits; a build with `blocked: "cap"` reads "Paused — monthly limit reached" → Credits `#credit-alerts`), Blog results, Messages (a new message in the Inbox, a new Request; read when opened there), project team conversations, the marketplace (a sale of your listing or copy, urgent; a bid valued at Get paid's demo rates, `lib/account/payout.ts`, shown as "0.5 ETH (about $1,875 at the demo rate)"; your auction ending within an hour), credits and the plan (at or under the low mark, bought credits expiring within the reminder days, the plan renewing, changing or ending within three days), a ready video take, and comments by others on projects you shared. `filter.ts` decides what is heard (kind switch, muted project, bid threshold, hidden words), the count and the pop-up gate; `popups.ts` the daily summary and browser pop-ups; `read.ts` + `read-store.ts` keep what the bell marked read in `ideeza:notifications:read`, beside the Blog's own seen state.
- **The bell** (`components/notifications/`): one list for the sidebar's bell, the phone's and the editors' top bar; it never leaves a notice out for a setting. Unread notices carry a dot; the count is unread notices that are heard, less messages and team conversations (those count on the Messages badge only) and less what came in during quiet hours. A notice that uses one of your hidden words (Privacy & safety) shows "Hidden: it uses a word you hide" and isn't heard. Read ones stay 30 days. The header's ⋯ has **Mark all as read** and **Notification settings**; a notice about a project has its own ⋯ **Mute this project**, with Undo in the panel.
- **Pop-ups**: the build attention toast and the Blog result toast follow the pop-up mode, quiet hours, the kind's switch, muted projects and hidden words. `notice-popups.tsx` (mounted at the root, editors included) shows a heard notice as the browser's own pop-up when the tab is hidden and the browser allows it (a build finishing on the chat open in that hidden tab included; tagged by notice, so two tabs show it once; a click opens it), and the once-a-day summary toast.

## Account
- **Page** `/settings/account` (`components/settings/account-page.tsx`, rows `lib/settings/rows/account.ts`): five cards, each one's id its search row. The Phase 1 Profile, Personal Information and Security pages are gone; their routes redirect here (Security to `#sign-in`).
- **Public profile** (`#public-profile`): your photo, name and headline as others see them, and **Edit profile**, which opens your profile with the Edit profile dialog open (`/creators/<handle>?edit=1`). Edit profile is the only editor of the public profile; Settings holds none of its fields.
- **Handle** (`#handle`): your profile's address as a link, **Copy** (the full address; the button reads "Copied"), and **Edit**: 3–30 lower-case letters, numbers or hyphens, checked as you type against the sample creators' handles, with the address it gives; empty keeps `/creators/me`; changing it says links to the old address stop working. Says the address opens your profile in this browser only until sign-in arrives.
- **Legal name** (`#legal-name`): private, one line (80), Edit → Save. Stored alone in `ideeza:account:identity` v2 (`lib/account/identity.ts`); a Phase 1 record's first and last name become it, and its gender, permanent address and shipping addresses are dropped on read. An invoice prints it under *Billed to* when Billing details has no name. For the identity check to use the same value, `useIdentity` reads it and `saveLegalName` writes it.
- **Wallet** (`#wallet`): the connected demo wallet's short address, its account and network, and the Testnet demo badge, else "Not connected"; **Manage** / **Connect wallet** opens the Demo wallet dialog.
- **Sign-in and security** (`#sign-in`): one line that sign-in isn't in the app yet (password, 2-step sign-in, devices and deleting the account come with it), that your work lives in this browser, and **Download your information** → `/settings/data#download`.
- **Shipping address**: not kept in Settings. No checkout ships a physical copy to you yet (a physical copy is bought only by a demo creator, through the wallet dialog), so nothing asks for one.


## Get paid
- **One page** `/settings/get-paid` (`components/settings/get-paid/get-paid-page.tsx`; Settings v2, V7), rows by `#` (`lib/settings/rows/get-paid.ts`): `balance`, `withdraw`, `methods`, `get-paid-in`, `identity`, `history`. `?from=<project page>` (the Earn ladder's Verify identity, Earn › Money's Withdraw in Get paid) adds **Back to {project}** (`lib/account/return-to.ts` takes only a project path). The Phase 1 routes redirect here keeping the query (`lib/account/get-paid-href.ts`): `/settings/kyc` → `#identity`, `/get-paid/balance` → `#balance`, `/get-paid/history` → `#history`, the two tax forms → the page.
- **Set up when you sell**: until one of your projects is listed (Main NFT or copies) or sold, and the record holds no method or withdrawal (`getPaidStageOf`), the page is one card saying what will happen, row by row, with nothing to fill in; only Verify now (physical copies ask it before any sale).
- **Balance** (`lib/account/payout.ts`, D3): available to withdraw, in dollars at the demo rates (labelled), split by project (`earnedByProject`: each project's net, its sales, a link to its Earn sales), less what was withdrawn. Your share of a sale: a Main share is all yours; a copy sale is split by who held the project when it sold, after the 2.5 % IDEEZA fee. One dollar rule for the app: `DEMO_USD_RATES` (USDT, USDC, IDZ $1; ETH and WETH $3,750; MATIC $0.50), stated under the balance.
- **Withdraw** (`withdraw-dialog.tsx`): one dialog asking only what's missing (`withdrawStepsOf`): ① Verify your identity (Level 1) the first time, ② where the money goes (your demo wallet's address or any address, its network, a name) the first time, ③ the amount (whole balance or another amount up to it) with the full address, network, token you're paid in, fee $0.00 and total. **Sign and withdraw** opens the demo wallet with the same rows (it asks to switch network if needed); one signature saves a first withdrawal's method as the default too. The balance is re-checked before it confirms; the withdrawal is recorded as complete with a reference (no coins move), then **See it in history**. Nothing to withdraw: Withdraw is greyed with the reason.
- **Withdrawal methods**: each with its network, full address, added date and the Default tag; **Add method** (`method-dialog.tsx`) and ⋯ **Make default** are signed in the demo wallet (purpose `payoutMethod`); ⋯ **Remove** confirms and names the new default. Every add, default change and removal is logged (`methodLog`) and shows in the bell as an urgent Coin notice (`payoutNotices`, "You added a withdrawal method … Not you? Remove it in Get paid"). Bank and PayPal aren't in the app: one line in the dialog says so.
- **Get paid in**: USDT or IDZ (demo rate 1 IDZ = $1), saved on the spot (D14).
- **Verify your identity** (`identity.tsx`, `lib/commerce/kyc.ts`): Level 1 is the demo check, full legal name and country, both required, no terms to agree to (V10). The name starts from and saves to your one private legal name (`lib/account/identity.ts`, shown in Settings › Account); the country isn't kept; the check keeps only `{ status: "verified", at }`. Verified shows the date and **Reset**. The higher checks ($1,000 and $5,000 of total sales) are one line, shown only from 75 % of a threshold (`higherCheckOf`), saying they aren't in the app yet so nothing is limited.
- **Withdrawal history**: every withdrawal newest first, ten to a page (amount and token, when, where with its network, reference, status); **Download statement** (year, a month or the whole year) downloads a CSV at once with your sales (your share), their IDEEZA fees and your withdrawals, in the coin and in dollars at demo rates, with a UTF-8 BOM so a spreadsheet shows Bangla names.
- **No tax data** (V7): the tax forms wait for real payouts. A tax residence or SSN/EIN digits an older record held are dropped on read and scrubbed from storage when the page opens (`payoutNeedsScrub`, `scrubPayout`).
- **Before Get paid**: payout requests a project's Earn made (`ideeza:commerce:payouts`) read as pending withdrawals ("Requested in Earn"), so none is lost.

## Language & region
`/settings/language` (`components/settings/language-page.tsx`, rows `lib/settings/rows/language.ts`). Every control saves as it changes.
- **App language**: one line, "English. Bangla is coming." No picker of languages the app doesn't have.
- **Voice typing language** (`language.voice`, `lib/settings/voice.ts`): Same as your browser (the default) maps `navigator.languages` onto the list ("bn" → Bangla (Bangladesh), an English the list lacks → American English), or one language by hand; every dictation box (`useVoiceInput`) starts its recogniser in it. The row says what the mic listens for and that the browser does the speech-to-text (Chrome sends the audio to Google).
- **Date and time format** (`language.dateLocale`, `lib/settings/regions.ts` + `dates.ts`): Automatic (the default, naming the region it found), every region the browser knows (built at runtime from `Intl`, A to Z, each with its live example of 12 March 2020 18:09), or ISO. A region's dates are written in English with its own order and clock: its own English when the browser has one (en-GB, en-IN…), else the English with the same order and clock (Bangladesh: day first, 12-hour, "19/10/2026, 11:23 pm"). Automatic follows a browser language that names a region ("bn-BD", "en-GB"), else the time zone's region. An older stored locale ("de-AT") reads as its region.
- **Time zone** (the profile's `timeZone` + `timeZoneManual`, `lib/people/time-zones.ts`): its only editor. This device (the default) or any IANA zone; the row shows the time there now. Every date and time `lib/settings/dates.ts` formats (History, Messages, Blog list, Billing history and invoices, Get paid) and the profile's local time use it; the bell's day and week headings stay on the device's calendar. Dates other screens still write themselves (Plan & billing's renewal dates, the PCB editor's saved time, a profile's Work list) use the device's zone.
- **The dates helper** (`lib/settings/dates.ts`, hook `use-dates.ts`): `formatDate` (numeric · medium · long · day · weekday), `formatTime`, `formatDateTime`, `formatStamp` (today in the chosen zone), all in the chosen format and zone; `timeZone: DEVICE_ZONE` keeps a day worked out on the device's calendar on it (the bell's day and week headings). Relative words ("now", "5m", "Today") stay as they are.

## Appearance & accessibility
`/settings/appearance` (`components/settings/appearance-page.tsx`, rows `lib/settings/rows/appearance.ts`). Every control saves as it changes.
- **Theme** Light · Dark · Match system: the app's one theme (`ideeza-theme`, `components/theme-provider.tsx`). The account menu's theme icons and ⌘K are shortcuts to the same value, so each shows the other's change at once; the PCB editor's View and Setting › System rows point here.
- **Text size** Default · Large, **High contrast**, **Reduce motion** Match system · Reduce: `<html data-text-size="large" | data-contrast="high" | data-motion="reduce">` (`lib/settings/appearance.ts`), set before the first paint and followed by `AppearanceSync`. Large steps each `--font-size-*` and its line height up one DS step. High contrast (`tokens.css`, both themes and inside the editors' shell): secondary and tertiary text take the primary ink, disabled and coloured text a darker (light) or lighter (dark) DS step, every border and a switch's off track a step that reads 3:1; measured on this page, text 10.4:1 or more (light) and 11.1:1 (dark), borders 4.8:1 and 7.3:1. Reduce joins the reduced-motion rule in `reset.css`; scripted motion asks `motionReduced()`.
- **Single-key shortcuts** (on by default; `lib/settings/keys.ts`): off, the PCB, 3D and Connection map editors ignore a key that types a character on its own; ⌘/Ctrl shortcuts and Escape, Delete, Space keep working. Under the switch, every such key per editor, listed from the tables the editors run (`lib/settings/shortcuts.ts`).
- **Default units** mm · mil · inch: the PCB editor's default for new boards and parts, set without a project open (`ideeza:pcb:appSettings` through the PCB store's `setDefaultMeasurementUnit`; an open board keeps its own unit). Grid, snap and the rest stay in the editor's Setting.
- **Data saver**: a post's video doesn't preload (not a byte until Play); videos never autoplay anyway.

## AI & your data
`/settings/data` (`components/settings/data-page.tsx`, `data-search-history.tsx`, `data-blog-reuse.tsx`, `data-copy.tsx`; the route reads this deployment's image and 3D keys at request time). Phase 1's AI Properties, Personal Data and Your activity redirect here.
- **Where your data goes** (`#where`): this browser (everything you make); IDEEZA's server (each concept image with its prompt, each problem report, kept with no end date and no delete yet; an image opens for anyone with its random address; prompts pass through, so the AI services don't see your IP); Pollinations (text prompts); AI Horde while the volunteer pool is on, or Pollinations images when the deployment has `POLLINATIONS_TOKEN`; Meshy (a concept image's address) only when `MESHY_API_KEY` is set; the browser's speech service for voice typing (Google's, in Chrome); Google Fonts and the editors' public file hosts (your IP, not your work).
- **Training** (`#training`, V9): "IDEEZA doesn't train AI on your designs." The services above keep their own rules. **Cookies** (`#cookies`): IDEEZA sets no cookies and has no analytics or advertising trackers.
- **AI**: *Use the volunteer GPU pool for images* (`#ai`, on by default): off, concept images and the 3D editor's AI Generate send `pool: "off"` and, with no other image service, are refused (`pool-off`, 409) with the reason on the card and in the 3D dialog; disabled with its reason when the deployment draws with Pollinations. *AI help in the editors* (`#editor-assist`): off hides every editor's AI assistant and the 3D toolbar's AI Generate.
- **Search history** (`#activity`, V12): *Keep my search history* (`privacy.keepSearchHistory`, off by default). On, ⌘K (when you open a result), Innovations and Explore marketplace searches are kept in `ideeza:activity` (`lib/data/activity-log.ts`: 90 days, 200 lines, typing one search is one line); the list shows by day (Innovations and Explore lines run the search again), ten first, then *Show all*. Off keeps nothing new; what is kept stays until **Clear search history** (confirmed). The old video watch log is gone: reading a record drops it, and this page rewrites a stored one without it.
- **Blog reuse** (`#blog-reuse`): every blog where you chose "Give access to IDEEZA to future use", with **Revoke** and, for two or more, **Revoke all** (confirmed); a blog IDEEZA already shared offers **Contact support** instead (`lib/data/blog-reuse.ts`).
- **Download your information** (`#download`): one button makes a zip in this browser (`lib/data/export-browser.ts`) and downloads it: a readable `index.html` per kind of information and one at the root (`export.ts`, `export-html.ts`; others' comments left out), project files (each PCB as `.kicad_pcb`, firmware as files, wiring and 3D as JSON), every photo, attachment, project media file and clip from IndexedDB at full size in its kind's folder, and `ideeza-backup/backup.json` (`backup-format.ts`, v1: every `ideeza` key as stored, every IndexedDB record with each file swapped for its path in the zip). It lists what each kind holds now and when you last downloaded (`ideeza:data:last-backup`, `lib/data/backup.ts`).
- **Keep this browser from clearing it** (`#storage`): whether the browser has promised not to clear the data on its own (`navigator.storage.persisted`), how much it holds of what it allows, and **Ask the browser** (`persist()`), which says so when the browser refuses.
- **Restore from a backup** (`#restore`): choose the zip (as made, or unzipped and re-zipped by another tool: deflate via `DecompressionStream`, a folder round it; `lib/data/zip-read.ts`). Every file's CRC is checked; a file that isn't a zip, a zip with no backup, one from a newer IDEEZA, or a damaged one is refused with what to do, and nothing changes. Otherwise a dialog shows what each kind holds in the backup beside what is here now, the number of files, and **Download what's here first**; **Replace with this backup** deletes IDEEZA's databases, writes the backup's, then replaces every `ideeza` key, and reloads to "Restored from your backup of …".
- **Erase IDEEZA from this browser** (`#erase`): **Download a copy first**, then **Erase** (confirmed, naming the last download and saying the server's concept images and reports stay): clears every `ideeza` key in localStorage and sessionStorage and deletes every `ideeza` IndexedDB database (`lib/data/browser-data.ts`; the app keeps nothing in Cache Storage or cookies), then reloads to "IDEEZA was erased from this browser". Both reloads write their result once more on `pagehide`, so a store holding old data in memory can't save it back.
