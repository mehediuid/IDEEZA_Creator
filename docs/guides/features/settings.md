# Settings

The account's Settings (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-settings-design.md`; Figma User Panel V2 "✅ Settings" `19140:152179`). Settings is about the person; project things stay on the project. Every page has its own URL and every row a deep link (`/settings/<page>#<row>`). Back to [README.md](README.md).

## Shell
- **Frame** (`components/settings/settings-frame.tsx`): the white sheet on the page frame with the settings side-nav, a hairline, and the page — breadcrumb Settings › Group › Page, the page title, then its cards. Below a 768 px page container the side-nav goes and a "Settings" link leads back to the list.
- **Side-nav** (`settings-nav.tsx`): General · Preferences · Audience as accordions (the group holding the current page opens itself), then Plan & Pricing, Policy & Privacy, Activity, Report; the current page is the outlined brand pill. Pages and their order come from `lib/settings/registry.ts`.
- **Cards and rows** (`settings-card.tsx`, `settings-choice.tsx`): a bordered card with its header action (Figma's Edit), rows of label + helper + control, link rows with a chevron, read-only fields ("No information" when empty), the Cancel / Update pair, and the Figma radio-list modal with options that can be disabled with their reason. Arriving at `#<row>` scrolls the row into view and outlines it once.
- **Preferences store** (`lib/settings/prefs.ts`, `use-settings.ts`): one record `ideeza:settings` (v1) for notifications, language, appearance, AI, audience, blocking, privacy and credit alerts; a broken field falls back alone to its default. Data with a home of its own stays there (messages, profile, payment token, theme, PCB settings).
- **Home** `/settings`: Find a setting (every page and row, `lib/settings/search.ts`; Enter opens the first), the four Most visited tiles (IDEEZA Pay, Blocking, AI Properties, Payment), and Looking for something else — the privacy policy and the terms read "Not available yet" because the app has neither page. On a phone the page list follows.
- **Security** `/settings/security`: every Figma row needs accounts, so the page says sign-in isn't in the app yet, lists what will be here, and links to Download your information.
- **Doors**: the account popover's Settings row; ⌘K "Settings", "Plan & pricing", "Wallet & tokens" (opens the wallet dialog) and, while typing, every matching settings row; the Plan & pricing breadcrumb's "Settings".

## Profile locking
- `/settings/profile-locking` (Figma `22550:47505`): card **Profile Private** › **Lock Your Profile** (saves at once to `audience.locked`), and under it **How locking works** from the profile's lock dialog in our words: people you don't work with see your name, photo and "This profile is locked", not your projects, blogs and activity; people on your project teams still see all of it; people can still find you and message you if your message rule lets them. Links: Who can message you, View as public. The rule the profile reads is `seesLockedProfile` (`lib/settings/audience.ts`); the profile draws the locked view.

## Profile & Share
- `/settings/profile-share` (Figma `22048:34433`, radio modal `22048:34563`), every "who can" a value button that opens the modal (Anyone · People you work with · Only me, D9: people you work with = your project teams) and saves on Update:
  - **Viewing & Sharing**: Who can add you to a project team · Who can see who you follow · Allow others to save and link your shared projects (toggle) · Show my location and local time on my profile (toggle, off by default) · View as public (→ `/creators/me?view=public`).
  - **Who can find you**: Who can follow you · Who can invite you to a team conversation · **Who can message you** (D13, its one home; the Messages gear opens `#message`): Anyone · People you work with or who own a copy or hold a share · No one new, written to Messages' own store (`allowNew`: everyone · known · nobody).
  - **Comments and quiet mode**: Who can comment on your shared projects · **Quiet mode** (Off · 24 hours · 7 days, "On until …" while on): no new message requests reach you and only people you work with can comment.
- The rules (`lib/settings/audience.ts`, tests `settings-audience-*.test.mjs`): `reachAllows`, `canComment`/`commentRuleOf` (quiet mode narrows to people you work with), `canFollow`, `canAddToTeam`, `canInviteToTeamConversation`, `canSeeFollowing`, `canSaveOrLink`, `seesLockedProfile`. In this demo nobody else can follow you, add you to a team or invite you to a team conversation, so those rules live in the lib with their tests and nothing calls them yet.
- Where they bite today: Messages' start check (`canStart` with the ties from `workTieOf`: on your team, holds a share, owns a copy; and your quiet mode) for `/messages/new`, `?as=` demo creators and a profile's Message button; the comment rule shows as a line above the composer on your own post ("Who can comment: Anyone. Change", or "Quiet mode is on until …, so only people you work with can comment."), since only you comment on your own post in this demo.

## Blocking
- `/settings/blocking` (Figma `22048:34661`), card **Manage Blocking**:
  - **Block users** › "Blocked Users List" (`22048:34824`): + Add Blocked User, Search Contact, Unblock. Add Blocked User → "Blocked New Users": search the people you've met (your Messages conversations, comment threads on your posts or that you wrote in, people who bought from you; `peopleMet`), "Search result for …", Block (Blocked once done), "No one you've met matches …". One blocked list: the Messages one, which a profile's Block writes too.
  - **Block message** › "Blocked Message List" (`23712:52073`): your conversations with someone you blocked, each with Unblock (unblocks them everywhere).
  - **Hide specific words** ⌄ (`22048:34705` / `34764`): the note, Write keyword (comma or Enter makes a chip, Backspace removes the last), "Remaining n of 500", Cancel / Save (Update once words are saved). A plain word matches the whole word in any case and its #tag; "#word" matches only the tag (`hiddenWordIn`). It hides someone else's comment on your shared project (you read "Hidden: it uses a word you hide", the count leaves it out, its writer still reads it) and a message request whose sender used one (out of Requests and its count, with "n requests use a word you hide. Show · Hidden words").
