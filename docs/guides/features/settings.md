# Settings

The account's Settings (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-settings-design.md`; Figma User Panel V2 "✅ Settings" `19140:152179`). Settings is about the person; project things stay on the project. Every page has its own URL and every row a deep link (`/settings/<page>#<row>`). Back to [README.md](README.md).

## Shell
- **Frame** (`components/settings/settings-frame.tsx`): the white sheet on the page frame with the settings side-nav, a hairline, and the page — breadcrumb Settings › Group › Page, the page title, then its cards. Below a 768 px page container the side-nav goes and a "Settings" link leads back to the list.
- **Side-nav** (`settings-nav.tsx`): General · Preferences · Audience as accordions (the group holding the current page opens itself), then Plan & Pricing, Policy & Privacy, Activity, Report; the current page is the outlined brand pill. Pages and their order come from `lib/settings/registry.ts`.
- **Cards and rows** (`settings-card.tsx`, `settings-choice.tsx`): a bordered card with its header action (Figma's Edit), rows of label + helper + control, link rows with a chevron, read-only fields ("No information" when empty), the Cancel / Update pair, and the Figma radio-list modal with options that can be disabled with their reason. Arriving at `#<row>` scrolls the row into view and outlines it once.
- **Preferences store** (`lib/settings/prefs.ts`, `use-settings.ts`): one record `ideeza:settings` (v1) for notifications, language, appearance, AI, audience, blocking, privacy and credit alerts; a broken field falls back alone to its default. Data with a home of its own stays there (messages, profile, payment token, theme, PCB settings).
- **Home** `/settings`: Find a setting (every page and row, `lib/settings/search.ts`; Enter opens the first), the four Most visited tiles (IDEEZA Pay, Blocking, AI Properties, Payment), and Looking for something else — the privacy policy and the terms read "Not available yet" because the app has neither page. On a phone the page list follows.
- **Security** `/settings/security`: every Figma row needs accounts, so the page says sign-in isn't in the app yet, lists what will be here, and links to Download your information.
- **Doors**: the account popover's Settings row; ⌘K "Settings", "Plan & pricing", "Wallet & tokens" (opens the wallet dialog) and, while typing, every matching settings row; the Plan & pricing breadcrumb's "Settings".

## Profile
- **Page** `/settings/profile` (`components/settings/profile/profile-settings.tsx`, Figma General | Profile `22048:28793`), title My Profile.
- **Profile Picture**: your photo with "PNG, JPG under 10 MB" (Edit profile's real limit). **Upload New Picture** opens Upload Profile Picture: drop a picture or choose one, see it cropped round as it will show, Update saves it to the same IndexedDB `ideeza-profile` photo as Edit profile, so the sidebar and your profile change at once. **Remove** takes it away (greyed when there is none).
- **Profile** card, Edit → form, Cancel / Update: **First Name** and **Last Name** (private, `ideeza:account:identity`, `lib/account/identity.ts`), **Handle** (3–30 lower-case letters, numbers or hyphens, checked as you type against the sample creators' handles; shows the address it gives, `/creators/<handle>`; empty keeps `/creators/me`), **Gender** (Male · Female · Prefer not to say · Custom with your own words), **Links** (your profile's links, up to five, the same list Edit profile edits). View mode is Figma's two-column grid; **Wallet Address** shows the connected demo wallet's short address with the Testnet demo badge, or "Not connected" with **Connect** (the wallet dialog). Email, phone and birthday wait for sign-in and are not shown.

## Personal Information
- **Page** `/settings/personal-information` (`components/settings/profile/personal-information.tsx`, `abilities.tsx`, `shipping.tsx`; Figma `22048:29733` and its state frames). Four cards, each with Edit (Cancel / Update; nothing saves before Update).
- **Profile Details**: **Bio** (your profile's headline, 60) and **About yourself** (its About, 280), the same fields Edit profile edits; **Permanent Address** (Country from every ISO country `Intl` names, `lib/account/countries.ts`; City; Address) and **Shipping Address**: up to five, each with Edit and Remove, **Add Shipping Address** / **Add Another Address** opening Figma's dialog (Address Name, Country, City, Address, "Use as my default shipping address"), one of them always tagged **Default & Shipping**, and at five Figma's notice instead of the add button. Addresses are private.
- **Abilities**: **Skills** as chips (type and press Enter or Add; "You can add up to 16 skills"), **Specialists (Top 3 Skills)**: tick up to three of your skills and choose 0-1 · 1-2 · 3-5 · 5+ Years.
- **Connected Accounts**: Facebook, X, Instagram, LinkedIn as profile URLs (a username becomes the URL; an address on another site is refused with how to fix it). View mode shows the account name as a link.
- **Additional Information**: **Timezone**, a list of every IANA zone with this device's first as "(this device)"; choosing another keeps it, and Edit profile no longer replaces it with the device's zone. **Language**, the languages you speak as chips, Add Another opening Figma's **Select Language** (names from `Intl`).
- Skills, top specialties, languages and social accounts show on your profile (see platform-and-projects.md › Creator profile).
