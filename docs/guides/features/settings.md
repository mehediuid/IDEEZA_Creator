# Settings

The account's Settings (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-settings-design.md`; Figma User Panel V2 "✅ Settings" `19140:152179`). Settings is about the person; project things stay on the project. Every page has its own URL and every row a deep link (`/settings/<page>#<row>`). Back to [README.md](README.md).

## Shell
- **Frame** (`components/settings/settings-frame.tsx`): the white sheet on the page frame with the settings side-nav, a hairline, and the page — breadcrumb Settings › Group › Page, the page title, then its cards. Below a 768 px page container the side-nav goes and a "Settings" link leads back to the list.
- **Side-nav** (`settings-nav.tsx`): General · Preferences · Audience as accordions (the group holding the current page opens itself), then Plan & Pricing, Policy & Privacy, Activity, Report; the current page is the outlined brand pill. Pages and their order come from `lib/settings/registry.ts`.
- **Cards and rows** (`settings-card.tsx`, `settings-choice.tsx`): a bordered card with its header action (Figma's Edit), rows of label + helper + control, link rows with a chevron, read-only fields ("No information" when empty), the Cancel / Update pair, and the Figma radio-list modal with options that can be disabled with their reason. Arriving at `#<row>` scrolls the row into view and outlines it once.
- **Preferences store** (`lib/settings/prefs.ts`, `use-settings.ts`): one record `ideeza:settings` (v1) for notifications, language, appearance, AI, audience, blocking, privacy and credit alerts; a broken field falls back alone to its default. Data with a home of its own stays there (messages, profile, payment token, theme, PCB settings).
- **Home** `/settings`: Find a setting (every page and row, `lib/settings/search.ts`; Enter opens the first), the four Most visited tiles (IDEEZA Pay, Blocking, AI Properties, Payment), and Looking for something else — the privacy policy and the terms read "Not available yet" because the app has neither page. On a phone the page list follows.
- **Security** `/settings/security`: every Figma row needs accounts, so the page says sign-in isn't in the app yet, lists what will be here, and links to Download your information.
- **Doors**: the account popover's Settings row; ⌘K "Settings", "Plan & pricing", "Wallet & tokens" (opens the wallet dialog) and, while typing, every matching settings row; the Plan & pricing breadcrumb's "Settings".

## Privacy
- `/settings/privacy` (Figma `22048:35155`, `components/settings/privacy-page.tsx`). Figma's "Social Share" card keeps only its last row, so it is called **Search**: the two Facebook rows wait for connected accounts (D15).
- **Link with search engine** (`privacy.searchEngines`, off by default): while off, your own profile page (`/creators/me`) carries `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` (`profile-robots.tsx`, mounted in the profile route); on, it carries none. Other creators' pages are untouched.
- **Blog reuse**: every blog where you ticked "Give access to IDEEZA to future use", newest grant first, each with **Revoke** (at once, said in a status line) and, with two or more, **Revoke all** (confirmed). A blog IDEEZA already shared can't be revoked here: its row says where and when and offers **Contact support** (Report a problem with the blog named). The rule is the blog's own (`lib/data/blog-reuse.ts` over `lib/blog/article.ts` `setReuse`).
- **Cookies and analytics**: a read-only statement that is true today: no cookies of the app's own, no analytics or advertising trackers; theme, settings and work live in this browser's storage; a tab's recent errors leave only with a problem report that has "Send page and device details" ticked.

## Personal Data
- `/settings/personal-data` (Figma `22048:35199`, `personal-data-page.tsx`): the **Download Your Information** row opens the Figma wizard in one dialog (`download-wizard.tsx`): Available Information (everything) or Specific Information → Choose Information (nine kinds, each with what this browser holds of it) → Choose method to save your data (Download to device) → Create Files to download with **Date Range** (All Time · Last Year · Last 6/3 Months · Last Month · Last Week · Custom with two dates), **Format** (HTML · JSON) and **Media Quality** (High: photos and files at full size and your videos · Medium: photos made smaller, no videos · Low: no media), each its own radio modal. Back on every step after the first; "Step n of m".
- **Create File to download** builds a `.zip` in the browser and downloads it (`ideeza-information-<date>.zip`), with progress while it reads photos and files; the page then says the name, size and file count. Nothing is sent anywhere and no store is written.
- **What's in it** (`lib/data/export.ts`, `export-html.ts`, `export-browser.ts`, `zip.ts`): every `ideeza…` key in one of Product · Marketplace · Coin (credits and payments) · Newsfeed (Innovations) · Blog · Message · Activity · Profile Information, plus **Project files** — each product's board as a real `.kicad_pcb` (the PCB exporter) beside the editor document, firmware files as files, wiring, 3D, assembly and preview as JSON. Interface state and the editors' backups stay out; a key nobody has named goes with Profile. Of a post's comments only yours and the ids you reported leave. A date range keeps each record's dated lines and drops an older project's keys and files with it; undated things (profile, settings) stay whole. HTML gives a readable `index.html` per kind and one at the root; JSON one file per record and a `manifest.json`. Photos, attachments, project media and clips come from IndexedDB through each store's own reader.
- The zip is "stored" (no compression) with CRC-32 and UTF-8 names, written without a dependency; it opens in Finder, Windows and `unzip`.
- Not in Figma's list: its second "Marketplace Information", "Send to Manufacturer" (no such data) and "Security & Login" (no accounts). "Notify" waits for sign-in; the file is ready at once, so there is no 72-hour window.
- A card under the row, **What your file holds**, lists the nine kinds with a line of what this browser holds of each.

## Your activity
- `/settings/activity` (Figma `22048:35243`, `activity-page.tsx`): the card **Actions you have done in IDEEZA** with four rows and their counts, each opening its list at `?list=appreciated|comments|videos|searches`. "Logged in history" needs sign-in and isn't shown.
- **Appreciated** (D16): posts you appreciated (`ideeza:innovations:reactions`), newest first and said to be without days, because an appreciation keeps no date. **Comments**: every comment you wrote across the posts' stored threads, by day, each linking to its comment.
- **Videos you have watched**: a post's video counts once it has played 3 s (`watch-recorder.tsx` round the post's video stage; skipping ahead doesn't count; the same video again that day moves up). **Your search history**: what you search on Innovations and Explore marketplace (typing one query is one line) and the ⌘K queries you open a result for, with where and what you opened; Innovations and Explore lines run the search again.
- Both logs live in `ideeza:activity` (`lib/data/activity-log.ts`, v1): 90 days, at most 200 lines each, newest first, grouped Today · Yesterday · date. **Clear watch history** and **Clear search history** are confirmed.

## Report
- `/settings/report` (Figma `22550:48543`, `report-page.tsx`): **Reports you made** lists every report this browser sent — profiles (`ideeza:people:reports`), posts and comments (their reported lists), messages (`ideeza:messages`) and Report a problem (its reference, now kept in `ideeza:support:sent`) — newest first, each opening to the reported words, reason, your note, reference and status: "Received, not reviewed yet", or for a problem report "Sent to the IDEEZA team". Post and comment reports keep no date or reason and say so.
- **Reports about your work**: nobody reviews reports in the app yet, so the card says nothing has been decided; the Figma's notices and Appeal arrive with reviews (Phase 2).
