# Features — the Earn layer

Part of the feature inventory ([index](README.md)). Keep it current: [AGENTS.md › Keeping docs current](../../../AGENTS.md#keeping-docs-current-required). Specs: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-11-build-earn-p0-design.md` (Build and Earn P0) and `…-p1-design.md` (P1). The project page (`/projects/[id]`) is engineering; everything that makes money from a project lives here.

## Earn area (`/projects/[id]/earn[/<tab>]`)
- **Routes** (`app/(create)/projects/[id]/earn/`): one `layout.tsx` (`components/projects/earn/earn-shell.tsx`) reads every store once through the project page's `useProjectPageData`, with the same viewer and `view.canCtx`; `page.tsx` is the Overview and `[section]/page.tsx` the other tabs (`sell` · `plan` · `money`). Moving between tabs keeps the header, the tabs and the read, and swaps only the tab body (`earn-context.tsx`). Every path comes from `lib/manual/earn-route.ts`: a part of a tab is the tab's path with its id as the hash (`earnHref(id, "customers", { sale })` → `/earn/money?sale=…#customers`), scrolled to once the tab renders. The old section addresses redirect on the server (`earnSectionRedirectOf`): `editions` → `sell#copies`, `splits` / `customers` / `rights` → `money#…`, a customers `sale=` kept. An unknown section answers 404, `/earn/overview` goes to `/earn`, and the old `/projects/[id]/business-plan` redirects to `/earn/plan`.
- **Layout** (`surface.tsx`, `earn-section.tsx`): the header and the tabs span the page frame on every tab (owner, 2026-11-18, D1 of the Sell desk plan), so moving between tabs moves nothing; Overview, Business plan and Money read in the narrow column (at most 720 px) under them, Sell is the frame with the rail (`WITH_RAIL`). One card style (`EARN_CARD`), one heading scale (an h2 per section with one plain sentence under it, an h3 inside), 32 px between sections; empty states are one line and one action, left-aligned. At most one violet control per tab (on Sell, per item panel).
- **Header** (`earn-header.tsx`): "← {project}" back to the project page, the h1 "Earn" and one status pill in words from the commercial axis (`earnStatusOf`: *Not for sale yet* · *Minted* · *On the marketplace* · *Listing paused* · *Part sold* · *Sold out* · *Given away*), the lock line once sold in full, and, once minted, **Preview as others see it**, a ghost link to `/projects/<id>?view=buyer`. Nothing in the header or the tabs prints.
- **Tabs** (`earn-nav.tsx`): Overview · Sell · Business plan · Money, page links with `aria-current="page"` drawn with the project page's tab-strip classes (`tab-strip.tsx`); one row that scrolls sideways at phone width (the edge it can scroll past fades, the current tab is brought into view), 44 px on a phone or a coarse pointer, never violet.
- **Owner only** (`canOpenEarn`): a preview (`?view=buyer`, `?view=contributor&as=`) keeps its banner and gets "Only the owner can manage earning" with a link back to the project page carrying the preview.
- **Focus**: arriving focuses the h1; a tab change keeps focus on the link and says the tab in the page's polite live region; the document title is "{tab} · Earn · {project} · IDEEZA".

## Tabs
- **Overview** (`overview.tsx`): the path ① Mint → ② Sell → ③ Get paid (`earnPathOf`), done steps ticked and the current one saying *You are here*, then one card for the current step (`earnNextOf`): its title and one sentence on why; while its commit point has steps missing, **Before you can mint** / **sell** lists only those, each with its one-tap fix (`LevelCheck` over `commitCheck`, the steps the project page's Earn button counts), and the step's own action (**Start mint**, **Choose what to sell** → the Sell tab), the tab's one violet control, shows only once none is missing. Once something is live (listed, paused, given, sold, or copies on sale without the Main NFT) the card says the state in plain words (a sale's share joins your balance in Settings › Get paid), a facts row (`earnLiveFactsOf`: price, copies sold of the copies made, whole project and products, *Bought by* (how many people, *Nobody yet* at none), and earned, the sales' payouts summed per token) and a link to where it's managed.
- **Sell** (`sell.tsx`, `sell/board.tsx`, `sell/item-panel.tsx`, `lib/market/sell-desk.ts`; the owner's selling rules 2026-10-11; the Sell desk, owner 2026-11-18, plan https://claude.ai/artifact/KG362uZ1MZyahtgV1ygmuD, direction A with every recommendation D1–D12): a sales desk across the page frame. **The board** (main column): *What you're selling* (*What you sold* once sold in full) with the Testnet demo badge and **How selling works**, a disclosure holding the three rule cards and their lead word for word (D2); the lead line; a line over the board (D6, `deskCaptionOf`: *n of m on sale · sold out · copies sold · earned*, earned being the Overview's own sum); then a row for the whole project and one per product (D3, `deskProductsOf`: a one-product project has one row, the project, naming the product, unless that product already has copy tracks of its own), a column for Main NFT, Physical NFT and Virtual NFT under heads that say each rule in a line, in two labelled groups (owner, 2026-11-18: a line alone didn't read as a divider): *Whole project — The Main NFT, and copies of everything in it.* and *One product at a time · n products — Products sell copies only, not a Main NFT.* A product's Main cell is an empty dashed cell. A row's name cell has the project's cover (a product's initials), what it is and what it earned. A **tile** (`mainTileOf`, `copyTileOf` over the rows' own `mainRowStateOf` / `copyRowStateOf`) is a chip (On sale, Auction, In checkout, Paused, Part sold, Sold out, Sold in full, Not selling, Not minted yet, Stopped, Given, Can't sell), its price (a range across two uses), its uses, a sold meter (copies sold of made; the Main NFT's share sold of 100), and its earnings, *Set a price ›* while not selling, or *n steps before listing* while the ladder misses a step for the Main NFT. A tile only picks (`aria-pressed`, D4). Before the mint one band stands in for the tiles: *Not minted yet · Mint {project} once…* (D10). Under the board: the note that selling the Main NFT sells the project (left out once it has), **Mint details** (with *Open the mint summary*), and *No products yet* with Open products. **The item panel** (the rail, sticky; under a 1024 px page — `useRailStacked` — a tap opens it from the side as the DS Drawer, full width on a phone, titled with the item, and each tile says what the tap does: *Manage ›*, *Sell ›*, *Mint ›*, *Receipt ›*, *Details ›*; owner, 2026-11-18: below the board it was far down the page. While a dialog its flows open sits on top, the drawer is `covered`, found by watching for a modal added after it opened): the tile's kind, title (*Physical copies of the whole project*, *Virtual copies of RefillTank*, the project's name for the Main NFT), its line and chip; then the tile's one home for every control the rows had: Main NFT — before the mint *Mint and sell the project* (the mint flow, sell intent), minted and not listed `ListingFlow` (*Sell the Main NFT*, violet; *Before you can list* while a ladder step is missing), listed or paused `ListingBody` (Edit · Remove · Relist · Bidding history · Close auction), sold in full a **Receipt** (price, bought by, you got, date, share; *See this sale in Money*, *Get paid*; D11); a copy tile — not selling **Sell {physical|virtual} copies** (the Sell copies flow, kind preset, violet), listed the numbers (Sold with its meter, Earned, Bought by, Last sale), *Every copy is sold…* when sold out, `EditionTracks` (Edit · Remove listing · Add copies) and *Sell … copies too*, blocked its reason (before the mint *Mint privately*). Then the NFT's **Activity** line (D9), **Recent sales** (the last three by the buyer's name, copy number and use, price and date; *All sales in Money*; D8) and **What buyers see**, the project's own Explore marketplace card (`ListingCard` over `marketItemsOf`) with **View on marketplace** while the tile is on sale (`marketHrefOf`; D7). Before the mint the panel offers the mint's two ways: *Mint and sell the project* and *Mint privately, sell copies later*. The tile open on arrival (`defaultTileOf`, D5): a copy track sold out, a buyer in checkout, an auction ending soon, a paused listing, the Main NFT not listed, else the tile sold most recently, else the Main NFT; `#copies` / `#editions-<product>` (the Overview's *Sell copies of a product*) open a product's copies, `?list=1` the Main NFT, whose ListingFlow then opens once. Whole-project copies are tracks with `productId` = `PROJECT_SCOPE`. The phone (`/m`) keeps its own rows → item screens.
- **Sell copies** (`sell/sell-flow-dialog.tsx`, `sell-steps.tsx`, `lib/market/sell-flow.ts`): "Sell {virtual|physical} copies of {the whole project|product}", the kind preset by the row, two steps with a "Step n of 2" track. **Copies and price**: Copies (only for a new track), Token, Price with *You get {price − 2.5 % fee} per copy*; a built product adds the required "How you'll make and ship it" note (≤ 1,000 characters) and three promises (lead time, refund, "I'm the seller"); **More options** (closed) holds Price with future updates (empty = the price), Use (Personal / Business, the first open one) and Royalties (default 10, *a share of resales, if marketplaces honour it*). `effectiveTermsOf` fills the empty ones for both the checks and the stored listing. **Review**: what, copies, price, network; the rules (the product's AI video, with Generate AI video on top; for the whole project *Each product has its AI video, and the project video is made*, its reason the first thing still missing, a product's fixed with Generate AI video and the project video's with **Make videos** (`openMakeVideos`); ownership; the make-and-ship note back on step 1; never the Main listing); the ladder check (L3 virtual, L4 physical); the ownership box while unconfirmed, the lazy-mint box and *nothing here promises you income*; then **Sign and sell**, the dialog's one violet control, which makes Create *n* lazy NFTs (`createEditions`, when new) then Sign the listing (`listEdition`); a listing left unsigned keeps the copies created and the flow picks up at the listing. Next checks only its step and focuses the field at fault; Back keeps everything; closing with anything entered asks first. The note and promises are stored per scope in `ideeza:project:edition-terms:<id>` (swept on delete).
- **Business plan** (`plan.tsx` over `business-plan/plan-page.tsx`'s `BusinessPlanPanel`): with no plan, "Business plan" and one line with **Generate business plan** (the Generate and Writing dialogs); a progress row while it writes (Show progress · Stop writing) or after it stopped (Continue writing), then the plan under its h2 with versions, Edit, Regenerate, Print or save as PDF and Delete (which lands back on the empty state); each plan section is an h3.
- **Money** (`money.tsx`), three sections: **Splits** (`#splits`): the ownership line (`ownershipRow`) over the roster with each share, the over-100 % warning, Add contributor and its add/edit dialog, removal, and Preview, which opens the project page as that contributor; empty, *No contributors yet.* and Add contributor. **Sales** (`#customers`, Testnet demo): once the project has a sale, **Your earnings** (`project-earnings.tsx`; Settings spec D3): what its sales paid you, your share after the fee and the splits, per coin and in dollars at the demo rates, and **Withdraw in Get paid** (`/settings/get-paid?from=…#withdraw`): earnings are one balance for the account, withdrawn only in Settings › Get paid; then the project's Sales panel; `?sale=<id>` opens and focuses that sale; empty, *No sales yet.* and, before a listing, Go to Sell. **Rights** (`#rights`): a list of rights, as many as the maker has (owner, 2026-10-13: `lib/manual/legal.ts` `rights[]`, each a Patent, Copyright or Trademark with an optional https link and a trademark's attorney; "Add a right", each row's Edit, Remove with a confirm; an old one-of-each record reads as up to three rights) — the legal details (patent, copyright, trademark, inline edit; **Add legal details** when there are none) and, for a project given under a license, the license.
- **The gating ladder** (`lib/commerce/ladder.ts`, spec P1-4): `earnLevelOf(facts)` → `{ level 0–4, next }`, cumulative: L1 Drafts needs something built (every project has a product), L2 Mint the demo wallet connected, ownership confirmed and the step *Videos ready* (every product's video, then the project video made from them), L3 Sell & editions minted (the commercial axis, `commercialStateOf`) and a license chosen; selling never asks for a withdrawal method (a sale lands in the Get paid balance; the first withdrawal asks where the money goes, Settings v2 V7). L4 Physical editions the demo identity check (withdrawing in Get paid asks it too). Engineering is never gated. The project page's Earn button counts the next commit point's steps (`commitStepsOf`). Every commerce commit point asks `useCommitCheck(point, …)` and shows the missing steps inline under *Before you can {commit}* (`LevelCheck`), the commit aria-disabled (or, on the Overview and Your project, left out) until they're done; a step the call site gates itself (its readiness gate, its ownership box) is left to it: the mint flow's **Sign** (L2 for Keep private and Give, L3 for a Sell, which lists as it mints; its Details asks the license), Add to marketplace / List another share and **Relist** (L3), the edition flow's **Sign** (L3 Virtual, L4 Physical). The fixes: Connect wallet (the Demo wallet dialog), Confirm ownership (writes `ownerConfirmedAt`), Make videos in Media, Go to mint (Earn › Sell), **Choose a license** (the Brief's list; writes the draft's license) and **Verify identity** (Settings › Get paid `#identity`, carrying `?from=` the page you were on; the settings page offers Back to {project}).
- **Verify identity** (`lib/commerce/kyc.ts`): Level 1, on Settings › Get paid (its Identity row and the first withdrawal), with its Reset; the check stores only `ideeza:commerce:kyc` = `{ status: "verified", at, demo: true }`, and the name typed is your private legal name (`lib/account/identity.ts`). Asked only by physical editions and by withdrawing in Get paid.
- **Selling a share** (`lib/commerce/share-copy.ts`): wherever a share of the project is sold (the listing fields in the mint flow and the listing dialog, the mint flow's review, the buyer's rail, the purchase and bid requests) the copy promises no return and carries the fixed note "Demo on a testnet. Selling a share of a project can be regulated as an investment where you live; this is not available for real money."; royalties read "*n*% · a share of resales, if marketplaces honour it".
- **Sold in full**: every section reads only, through the moved components' own `can(viewer, …, view.canCtx)` and lock: no listing start, the edition cards' sold-in-full note (no rows), the plan's Edit, Regenerate and Delete disabled, no Add contributor, no Edit legal details.
