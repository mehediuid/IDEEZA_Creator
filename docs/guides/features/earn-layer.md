# Features — the Earn layer

Part of the feature inventory ([index](README.md)). Keep it current: [AGENTS.md › Keeping docs current](../../../AGENTS.md#keeping-docs-current-required). Specs: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-11-build-earn-p0-design.md` (Build and Earn P0) and `…-p1-design.md` (P1). The project page (`/projects/[id]`) is engineering; everything that makes money from a project lives here.

## Earn area (`/projects/[id]/earn[/<section>]`)
- **Routes** (`app/(create)/projects/[id]/earn/`): one `layout.tsx` (`components/projects/earn/earn-shell.tsx`) reads every store once through the project page's `useProjectPageData`, with the same viewer and `view.canCtx`; `page.tsx` is the overview and `[section]/page.tsx` the rest. Moving between sections keeps the header, the sub-navigation and the read, and swaps only the section body (`earn-context.tsx`). Every path comes from `lib/manual/earn-route.ts`; an unknown section answers 404 and `/earn/overview` goes to `/earn`. The old `/projects/[id]/business-plan` redirects to `/earn/plan`.
- **Header** (`earn-header.tsx`): "← {project}" back to the project page, the h1 "Earn" and the commercial status chip, the status line (in the owner's Sold line the buyer links to their row in Customers, `?sale=`), the lock line once sold in full, and **Preview as buyer**, which opens `/projects/<id>?view=buyer`. Nothing in the header or the sub-navigation prints.
- **Sub-navigation** (`earn-nav.tsx`, from `EARN_GROUPS`): links with `aria-current="page"`; a 200 px left column with group micro-labels from a 1024 px page container, a sideways-scrolling strip with group hairlines below it (the edge it can scroll past fades, the current row is brought into view). Rows are 44 px, 36 px beside the page on a fine pointer; never violet.
- **Owner only** (`canOpenEarn`): a preview (`?view=buyer`, `?view=contributor&as=`) keeps its banner and gets "Only the owner can manage earning" with a link back to the project page carrying the preview.
- **Rail blocks are the content here**: the Marketplace, Outcome, Editions and Legal blocks sit on the rail's one surface (bordered from a 1024 px page container, hairline-topped below), held to 720 px; on a phone they stay open instead of folding into disclosures (`RailBlocksUnfolded`, rail-block.tsx).
- **Focus**: arriving focuses the h1; a section change keeps focus on the link and says the section in the page's polite live region; the document title is "{section} · Earn · {project} · IDEEZA".

## Sections
- **Overview** (`overview.tsx`): "Ready to earn" is the gating ladder's track (`ladder/ladder-track.tsx`): the five levels (Engineering · Drafts · Mint · Sell & editions · Physical editions & payouts), each Reached, Next or Locked in words and a glyph (five columns topped with bars from a 640 px section, one row per level below it), then the next level's steps, done or not, each missing one with its one-tap fix, and, once verified, "Identity verified (demo)" with **Reset identity check** — and one link card per way to earn (Sell the project, Sell editions, Business plan, Splits, Customers) with its gate badge, Wallet, *Wallet · ID for physical* (Sell editions) or No wallet (`EARN_OPTIONS`). No violet.
- **Project NFT** (`sell.tsx`): the Brief entry (Add Brief · Continue Brief · View brief) and Add to marketplace / List another share (`ListingFlow`), from `earnPairOf` — the header's old `nextAction` pair without Review version, so a draft with a version waiting still gets its Brief entry. `?list=1` opens the listing flow once on arrival, never before the ladder's check has run (so a full page load doesn't flash the dialog), and the address drops it. Below, on one rail surface, the Marketplace block (listing state, Edit · Remove · Relist · Bidding history · Close auction, the NFT type tabs) and the Outcome block.
- **Editions** (`editions.tsx`): the Testnet demo line, then one row per product (`#editions-<productId>`): its name and one line per listed track (*Design files · 3 of 30 sold · 0.05 MATIC*, *Built product · Sold out*), or *Not selling*, and one quiet **Sell copies** button that opens the flow. A gate replaces the button with the reason in plain words (`entryStateOf`, first match wins: not minted, which links to the mint flow, Given, sold in full, an unknown collection, a dropped product, then no live or paused Main listing, which links to Project NFT), and *Already on sale* once every kind and use is listed. A product with tracks has a native **Manage** disclosure holding each kind's existing rows (`EditionTracks`): Add to marketplace, Edit, Remove listing, Add copies. Nothing on the page is violet. A dropped product is listed only while it has editions.
- **Sell copies** (`sell/sell-flow-dialog.tsx`, `sell-steps.tsx`, `lib/market/sell-flow.ts`): "Sell copies of {product}", three steps with a "Step n of 3" track. **What are you selling?**: Design files (virtual: *buyers get a licence and download the files*) or Built product (physical: *you make each one and ship it*), a kind with no open use disabled as *Already on sale*. **Copies and price**: Copies (only for a new track), Token, Price with *You get {price − 2.5 % fee} per copy*; a built product adds the required "How you'll make and ship it" note (≤ 1,000 characters) and three promises (lead time, refund, "I'm the seller"); **More options** (closed) holds Price with future updates (empty = the price), Use (Personal / Business, the first open one) and Royalties (default 10, *a share of resales, if marketplaces honour it*). `effectiveTermsOf` fills the empty ones for both the checks and the stored listing. **Review**: what, copies, price, network; the edition rules (Main listing live or paused with a Project NFT link, the product's AI video with Generate AI video on top, ownership, the make-and-ship note back on step 2); the ladder check (L3 design files, L4 built product); the ownership box while unconfirmed, the lazy-mint box and *nothing here promises you income*; then **Sign and sell**, the dialog's one violet control, which makes Create *n* lazy NFTs (`createEditions`, when new) then Sign the listing (`listEdition`); a listing left unsigned keeps the copies created and the flow picks up at the listing. Next checks only its step and focuses the field at fault; Back keeps everything; closing with anything entered asks first. The note and promises are stored per product in `ideeza:project:edition-terms:<id>` (swept on delete).
- **Business plan** (`plan.tsx` over `business-plan/plan-page.tsx`'s `BusinessPlanPanel`): "No business plan yet" with Generate business plan (the Generate and Writing dialogs), a progress row while it writes (Show progress · Stop writing) or after it stopped (Continue writing), then the plan with versions, Edit, Regenerate, Print or save as PDF and Delete (which lands back on the empty state).
- **Splits** (`money.tsx`): the ownership line (`ownershipRow`) over the roster with each share, the over-100 % warning, Add contributor and its add/edit dialog, removal, and Preview, which opens the project page as that contributor.
- **Customers**: once the project has a sale, **Payouts** (`ladder/request-payout.tsx`): what its sales paid you and what isn't requested yet, per token, and **Request payout (demo)** (L4), which stores one request in `ideeza:commerce:payouts` and sends nothing, with the last request under it. Then the project's Customers panel; `?sale=<id>` opens and focuses that sale.
- **Rights**: the Legal information block (patent, copyright, trademark, inline edit) and, for a project given under a license, the license line.
- **The gating ladder** (`lib/commerce/ladder.ts`, spec P1-4): `earnLevelOf(facts)` → `{ level 0–4, next }`, cumulative: L1 Drafts needs something built (every project has a product), L2 Mint the demo wallet connected, ownership confirmed and every product's video ready, L3 Sell & editions minted (the commercial axis, `commercialStateOf`), a license chosen and the payout wallet (every mint has one), L4 Physical editions & payouts the demo identity check. Engineering is never gated. The project page's Earn button counts the next commit point's steps (`commitStepsOf`). Every commerce commit point asks `useCommitCheck(point, …)` and shows the missing steps inline (`LevelCheck`), the commit aria-disabled until they're done; a step the call site gates itself (its readiness gate, its ownership box) is left to it: the mint flow's **Sign** (L2 for Keep private and Give, L3 for a Sell, which lists as it mints; its Details asks the license), Add to marketplace / List another share and **Relist** (L3), the edition flow's **Sign** (L3 Virtual, L4 Physical) and **Request payout** (L4). The fixes: Connect wallet (the Demo wallet dialog), Confirm ownership (writes `ownerConfirmedAt`), Make videos in Media, Go to mint and Set payout wallet (Project NFT), **Choose a license** (the Brief's list; writes the draft's license) and **Verify identity**.
- **Verify identity (demo)** (`ladder/verify-identity-dialog.tsx`, `lib/commerce/kyc.ts`): a name and a country, both required and neither stored (autofill off), under "Demo — no real verification"; it stores only `ideeza:commerce:kyc` = `{ status: "verified", at, demo: true }`. Asked only by physical editions and Request payout; Reset removes it.
- **Selling a share** (`lib/commerce/share-copy.ts`): wherever a share of the project is sold (the listing fields in the mint flow and the listing dialog, the mint flow's review, the buyer's rail, the purchase and bid requests) the copy promises no return and carries the fixed note "Demo on a testnet. Selling a share of a project can be regulated as an investment where you live; this is not available for real money."; royalties read "*n*% · a share of resales, if marketplaces honour it".
- **Sold in full**: every section reads only, through the moved components' own `can(viewer, …, view.canCtx)` and lock: no listing start, the edition cards' sold-in-full note (no rows), the plan's Edit, Regenerate and Delete disabled, no Add contributor, no Edit legal details.
