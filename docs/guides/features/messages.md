# Features — Messages

Part of the feature inventory ([index](README.md)). Keep it current: [AGENTS.md › Keeping docs current](../../../AGENTS.md#keeping-docs-current-required). Design: spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-13-messages-design.md` (owner decisions D1–D10, 2026-10-13).

## Messages (`/messages`, `lib/messages`, `components/messages`)
- **People talk to people, nowhere else.** The AI concept chat, the bell, Activity and buying keep their own homes: a conversation's card has **Open**, never Buy; there is no offer and no broadcast. The bell lists a new message or Request as a notice (Settings › Notification, Message kind), read once the conversation is opened here.
- **Demo identities, this browser only.** Every user is a creator: You, the demo creators Mira, Leo and Sam, every project's team member, and the Innovations creators someone has written to. There is no switch on the page (owner, 2026-10-13): you are You, and only a `?as=` link (Explore marketplace's **Ask the creator**, as the demo creator you're shopping as) acts as someone else for that tab (`sessionStorage`), with a line saying so and **Back to you**. Demo-only: it leaves at the production cut.
- **Demo conversations** (`lib/messages/demo-seed.ts`, planted once by the sidebar so its badge counts them): Sora Min about AR caption glasses (read), Diego Alvarez about the desk occupancy tracker (his answer unread), Naim Rahman's hexapod tester request in Requests, and, with a project in this browser, Mira asking about your latest project's listing. A browser that already holds them gets nothing more.
- **A conversation is two people and one subject** (a project or an Innovations post); a project's team has one group conversation (you + its current team). A context card on top says what it is about (the listing, a purchase, the team, the post), and a new context is written into the timeline when it changes.
- **Where conversations start** (`/messages/new`, which finds or drafts it): the buyer rail's **Ask the creator** / **Get creator support** (a holder; replaces the old support dialog), Earn › Money › Sales' **Message {Name}**, the Team tab's **Message** and **Team conversation**, and **Message** beside Follow in an Innovations post's maker block (not on your own post). There is no free "New message".
- **Inbox and Requests.** A listing question, a purchase, a team, or someone who works with you (on your team, holds a share, owns a copy) lands in the Inbox; anyone else lands in Requests (**Accept** · **Delete** · **Block**). Only Accept accepts: a reply stays in Requests ("Replying keeps it here. Accept moves it to your Inbox.") and tells the sender nothing more than the reply.
- **Your rules live in Settings** (settings spec D13): the gear opens Settings › Privacy & safety › **Who can message you** (Anyone · People you work with or who own a copy or hold a share · No one new; questions about your listings still reach you under the middle one; No one new lets in only people you already have an Inbox conversation with) and **Take a break** (no new request reaches you until it ends; Requests says "You're taking a break until …" with Change). A refused start says why ("This creator has paused new message requests for now."). The blocked list is Settings › Privacy & safety › Blocked people, which names your conversations with each person. **Hidden words** (same page) take a request whose sender used one out of Requests and its count, with "n requests use a word you hide. Show · Hidden words". Your settings are yours: a demo creator this tab acts as has none.
- **Names open profiles:** the conversation header's name links to that person's profile (`/creators/<handle>`, the creator profile); a profile's Block and Settings › Privacy & safety write the same blocked list.
- **The list:** search (people, subject, text), All · Unread · Archived, a Project filter once there are two subjects; rows with the subject and how the other person stands to it, from your side (their project · Editor · holds 10% · owns a copy; nobody is a buyer or customer), the header saying it in full (Made Smart Lamp · Owns a Virtual copy of VoxLamp · Asked about your listing); the Project filter split into Your projects and Others' projects; the last line, the time and an unread dot. **The sidebar's Messages badge** counts unread Inbox conversations (not archived or muted) for the tab's person, and the tab title reads "(2) Messages".
- **The conversation:** day dividers, bubbles, Edited and Message deleted (own messages, 15 minutes; Delete asks first), photos and PDFs (≤ 10 MB, ≤ 5, IndexedDB `ideeza-messages`), project cards, Mute · Archive · Block · Report (a report is saved; nobody reviews it in the demo; a reported message is copied into the report, so you keep reading it as you reported it, with a line saying so, even after its sender edits or deletes it, and its files stay), a safety line and a scam hint under a received message that names a wallet, a seed phrase or paying outside the marketplace.
- **The old support requests** (`ideeza:market:support`) became conversations once, as the buyer's first message after a purchase context.
- **Store:** one `localStorage` key, `ideeza:messages`, read-modify-write on the freshest copy so two tabs don't drop each other's messages; an unreadable key is never written over.
- **Times follow Settings › Language › Date & Time Formatting**: the list's dated times ("12 Oct", "12 Oct 2025"), the day dividers past Yesterday and the clock under each group (`shortTimeOf` / `dayLabelOf` / `clockOf` with a format; "now", "5m", "Today" stay as words).
