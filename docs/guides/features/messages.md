## Messages (`/messages`, `lib/messages`, `components/messages`)
- **People talk to people, nowhere else.** The AI concept chat, the bell, Activity and buying keep their own homes: a conversation's card has **Open**, never Buy; there is no offer, no broadcast, and the bell never counts a message.
- **Demo identities, this browser only.** Every user is a creator: You, the demo creators Mira, Leo and Sam, every project's team member, and the Innovations creators someone has written to. **Acting as** (per tab, `sessionStorage`; any `?as=` sets it) switches who you are, so two tabs can talk live. Demo-only: it leaves at the production cut.
- **A conversation is two people and one subject** (a project or an Innovations post); a project's team has one group conversation (you + its current team). A context card on top says what it is about (the listing, a purchase, the team, the post), and a new context is written into the timeline when it changes.
- **Where conversations start** (`/messages/new`, which finds or drafts it): the buyer rail's **Ask the creator** / **Get creator support** (a holder; replaces the old support dialog), Earn › Money › Sales' **Message {Name}**, the Team tab's **Message** and **Team conversation**, and the creator row on an Innovations post. There is no free "New message".
- **Inbox and Requests.** A listing question, a purchase or a team lands in the Inbox; anyone else lands in Requests (**Accept** · **Delete** · **Block**; replying accepts). **Message settings**: who can start a conversation with you (Anyone · Only people you work with) and the blocked list.
- **The list:** search (people, subject, text), All · Unread · Archived, a Project filter once there are two subjects; rows with the subject and how the other person stands to it, from your side (their project · Editor · holds 10% · owns a copy; nobody is a buyer or customer), the header saying it in full (Made Smart Lamp · Owns a Virtual copy of VoxLamp · Asked about your listing); the Project filter split into Your projects and Others' projects; the last line, the time and an unread dot. **The sidebar's Messages badge** counts unread Inbox conversations (not archived or muted) for the tab's person, and the tab title reads "(2) Messages".
- **The conversation:** day dividers, bubbles, Edited and Message deleted (own messages, 15 minutes; Delete asks first), photos and PDFs (≤ 10 MB, ≤ 5, IndexedDB `ideeza-messages`), project cards, Mute · Archive · Block · Report (a report is saved; nobody reviews it in the demo), a safety line and a scam hint under a received message that names a wallet, a seed phrase or paying outside the marketplace.
- **The old support requests** (`ideeza:market:support`) became conversations once, as the buyer's first message after a purchase context.
- **Store:** one `localStorage` key, `ideeza:messages`, read-modify-write on the freshest copy so two tabs don't drop each other's messages; an unreadable key is never written over.
