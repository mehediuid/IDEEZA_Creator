## Tour Guide (`components/tour`, `lib/tour`)
- **Welcome** (`welcome-dialog.tsx`, DS M07 Modal): on Home, once, to a browser with no projects and no chats (or after Reset tours). The path from idea to product as five stages on one thread, each with its icon and one line of what happens there; **Show me around** (primary, with an arrow) runs Start here, **I'll explore on my own** (ghost) or ✕, Escape, the scrim close it for good and it says where the tour lives. A bottom sheet on a phone.
- **Chapters** (`lib/tour/chapters.ts`), the Figma's Dashboard Homepage Tour in parts of at most five steps:
  - **Start here** (Home): the prompt box, With AI or by hand, examples and Enhance, what every build gives, My projects.
  - **Find your way** (sidebar): Home, History, Parts & agile module, Explore marketplace + Innovations, Blog + Messages.
  - **Your account** (sidebar footer): profile, credits (the prices read from `CONCEPT_COST` / `BUILD_COST`), level, wallet, Tutorial.
  - **From idea to concepts** (a concept chat), **tips** rather than steps: each shows the moment its part of the chat is on screen — the questions (once they are asked, not while the AI reads), the first finished concept card, Build (its price from `BUILD_COST`), Save Project — with **Got it** and **Turn off tips**. Using the control under a tip (answering, pressing Build) counts it as seen; a tip never opens while someone is typing. On for a browser's first chat, or when turned on from the panel.
  - **Your project** (`/projects/<id>`): the tab strip, a product card, Earn, the ⋯ menu.
  - **The editors** (any editor module, the Figma's Pro pages tour): the rail's PCB Design, Code, 3D Module, Assembly (the Figma's missing 5/8), Product Preview.
  - **Inside the PCB editor**: the Properties panel, the premium parts price ($0.00 while every library part is free), the save status, Help.
  - **Mint and sell** (a project's Earn, any tab but the mint flow): the two-step path, the Mint step, Sell (IDEEZA's cut read from `IDEEZA_FEE_BPS`), Money.
  - **Share with the community** (`/innovations`): the feed's modes and categories together, a post card, then My projects for ⋯ › Share to Innovations (mint and the videos first).
  - On a phone the sidebar is a drawer, so Find your way and Your account are one step each on the menu button, and Start here's last step points there too. The editors' chapters aren't offered on a phone.
- **First-visit offer** (`chapter-offer.tsx`, spec D2): the first time a project page, an editor, Earn or Innovations opens, a small card rises into the corner (above the editors' status bar): a compass tile, "New here? Take a quick tour", the chapter and its step count, **Show me** and **Not now** — without dimming the page or taking focus. The editors before Inside the PCB editor; one offer per chapter, never again after Not now or once started; off with the panel's switch.
- **A step** (`tour-step.tsx`): one layer for a whole chapter. The page sits under the app's dialog scrim (an SVG path) with the step's controls cut out — a rounded hole 6 px clear of them that follows the control's own corners (a pill stays a pill), ringed in the focus colour; the control itself still works. Beside it, a 320 px card (16 px inside, the DS surface, border, radius lg and Elevation 3) with M10's arrow, placed by `@floating-ui/dom` (every side tried before it overflows) and kept there through scrolls and resizes: "n of N" and **Skip Tour Guide** (neutral link), the title (16/24 semibold), the body (14/20), then **Previous** (ghost) and **Next Tip** (primary, with an arrow); the last step says **Explore**, with "Next part: … →" to the next chapter. On a phone the card sits along the foot of the screen. Motion: the scrim and card fade in once (the card from the control's side); between steps the hole and the card glide together (300 ms, the standard curve) while the words cross-fade, and scrolling never animates; an ending fades out (100 ms); under reduced motion, or the Appearance setting, every change is immediate. Each step takes focus on its title and is announced ("Step 2 of 5: …"). ← → move, Escape skips; keys typed in the popup stop there. A control that isn't on screen is waited for (6 s for a run's first step, which may be waiting on its page; 1.5 s after), then skipped; a control that goes away, or a dialog opening over the page, pauses the chapter.
- **Targets**: `data-tour` ids on the real controls — `home-composer`, `home-mode`, `home-examples`, `home-get` (Home); `nav-*`, `plan`, `account`, `wallet`, `support-tutorial`, `support-tour`, `menu` (sidebar and phone bar); `chat-question`, `chat-concept`, `chat-build`, `chat-save` (the chat); `project-tabs`, `project-product`, `project-earn`, `project-actions` (project page); `rail-pcb` … `rail-preview` (the editors' rails), `pcb-properties`, `pcb-price`, `editor-saved`, `editor-help`; `earn-path`, `earn-step-mint` / `-sell`, `earn-overview` / `-sell` / `-plan` / `-money` (Earn); `feed-modes`, `feed-categories`, `feed-card` (Innovations). The first one on screen counts.
- **Tour Guide panel** (`tour-guide-panel.tsx`): Support › Tour Guide opens it beside the sidebar (the footer popovers' `FloatingPanel`); ⌘K "Open Tour Guide" and the phone's menu open it as a dialog (a sheet on a phone). First **Your journey** — Idea · Concept · Build · Edit · Earn on the DS **M33 Stepper** (`components/ideeza/stepper.tsx`, Horizontal · Dotted), each stage ticked by something actually done (`lib/tour/journey.ts`: an idea sent or a project started; a concept drawn or a build saved; a project saved; a product opened in an editor; a project minted), the first one not done marked as now (D7). Then the chapters under **Start**, **Build** and **Earn & share**: each row is one quiet button — a status mark (✓ seen, ▶ part-way, ○ not yet), the name, its steps and place, and **Start**, **Replay** or **Resume n/N** with an arrow — or, when it can't start yet, a lock, why ("Make your first project to open this.", "Send your first idea from Home to see these.", "Open IDEEZA on a computer to see the editors.") with **Go to Home** where that helps and Home isn't already open (D6). Then **Offer a tour the first time I open a page** (the DS Toggle) and **Reset tours**, which clears it all and brings the Welcome back on Home. A chapter started from another page goes to its page first: Home, the project on screen or the one changed last, that project's PCB editor, the latest chat (tips are turned on and show there), that project's Earn, Innovations.
- **Record**: `ideeza:tour` (`lib/tour/state.ts`, read live by `use-tour.ts`) — the Welcome's answer, each chapter's state (done, skipped, paused) and step, whether first-visit offers are on, and the offers answered with Not now. Which page is which, the offers' and tips' rules and each chapter's readiness are `lib/tour/places.ts`.
- **Ways in**: the Welcome, Support › Tour Guide (live; Help Center is still "not available yet"), ⌘K › Resources › Open Tour Guide.
