## Tour Guide (`components/tour`, `lib/tour`)
- **Welcome** (`welcome-dialog.tsx`, DS M07 Modal): on Home, once, to a browser with no projects and no chats (or after Reset tours). The path from idea to product as five stages on one thread, each with its icon and one line of what happens there; **Show me around** (primary, with an arrow) runs Start here, **I'll explore on my own** (ghost) or ✕, Escape, the scrim close it for good and it says where the tour lives. A bottom sheet on a phone.
- **Chapters** (`lib/tour/chapters.ts`), the Figma's Dashboard Homepage Tour in parts of at most six steps. **Every step names its page** (`page`, a `PageRef`): Next opens that page first, then lights the step there — a sidebar row is lit together with the page it opened (two targets, one hole each):
  - **Start here** (Home): the prompt box, With AI or by hand, examples and Enhance, what every build gives, then My projects opens (its row and the page's header).
  - **Find your way** (sidebar): History, Parts & agile module, Explore marketplace, Innovations, Messages, Blog — each opens, its row and its page header lit together.
  - **Your account** (sidebar footer): your profile (`/creators/me`, the row and the profile's head), credits (Plan & pricing, opened on its Credits tab; the prices read from `CONCEPT_COST` / `BUILD_COST`), your level (the step opens the account menu itself and closes it when the tour moves on), the wallet (`/balance`, the balance card), Tutorials.
  - **From idea to concepts** (a concept chat), **tips** rather than steps: each shows the moment its part of the chat is on screen — the questions (once they are asked, not while the AI reads), the first finished concept card, Build (its price from `BUILD_COST`), Save Project — with **Got it** and **Turn off tips**. Using the control under a tip (answering, pressing Build) counts it as seen; a tip never opens while someone is typing. On for a browser's first chat, or when turned on from the panel.
  - **Your project** (`/projects/<id>`): the tab strip, Earn, the ⋯ menu, then a product's page opens for **Open in editor**.
  - **The editors** (the Figma's Pro pages tour): each step opens its module of the project's product — PCB Design, Code, 3D Module, Assembly (the Figma's missing 5/8), Product Preview — and lights its rail button.
  - **Inside the PCB editor** (the PCB module): the Properties panel, the premium parts price ($0.00 while every library part is free), the save status, Help.
  - **Mint and sell** (the project's Earn): Overview's two-step path and its Mint step, then **Sell** opens (its tab and "What you can sell"; IDEEZA's cut read from `IDEEZA_FEE_BPS`), then **Money** opens (its tab and Sales).
  - **Share with the community**: `/innovations` (the feed's modes and categories), then the first post on the feed opens (its media and head), then the project page for ⋯ › Share to Innovations.
  - On a phone the sidebar is a drawer: a step still opens its page and lights the page's part (the row in the drawer isn't on screen). The editors' chapters aren't offered on a phone.
- **First-visit offer** (`chapter-offer.tsx`, spec D2): the first time a project page, an editor, Earn or Innovations opens, a small card rises into the corner (above the editors' status bar): a compass tile, "New here? Take a quick tour", the chapter and its step count, **Show me** and **Not now** — without dimming the page or taking focus. The editors before Inside the PCB editor; one offer per chapter, never again after Not now or once started; off with the panel's switch.
- **A step** (`tour-step.tsx`): one layer for a whole chapter. The page sits under the app's dialog scrim (an SVG path) with the step's controls cut out — a rounded hole 6 px clear of them that follows the control's own corners (a pill stays a pill), ringed in the focus colour; the control itself still works. Beside it, a 320 px card (16 px inside, the DS surface, border, radius lg and Elevation 3) with M10's arrow, placed by `@floating-ui/dom` (every side tried before it overflows) and kept there through scrolls and resizes: "n of N" and **Skip Tour Guide** (neutral link), the title (16/24 semibold), the body (14/20), then **Previous** (ghost) and **Next Tip** (primary, with an arrow); the last step says **Explore**, with "Next part: … →" to the next chapter. On a phone the card sits along the foot of the screen. Motion: the scrim and card fade in once (the card from the control's side); between steps the hole and the card glide together (300 ms, the standard curve) while the words cross-fade, and scrolling never animates; an ending fades out (100 ms); under reduced motion, or the Appearance setting, every change is immediate. Each step takes focus on its title and is announced ("Step 2 of 5: …"). ← → move, Escape skips; keys typed in the popup stop there. A step on another page navigates there first (the card fades, the new page's controls are waited for, then it lands); a step whose page can't be named (no project yet, no post on the feed) is skipped. A control that isn't on screen is waited for (6 s after a page change or for a run's first step; 1.5 s after), then skipped; a step with two targets waits briefly for both, then lights what is there. A step with `reveal` presses that control when a target isn't there — the account menu for the level (closed again when the tour moves on, and the card takes focus back), the Credits tab on Plan & pricing. The tour layer sits one level above popovers (`z-toast`), so an opened menu is dimmed with only its lit part cut out; beside a sidebar panel the card stands clear of the whole panel. A hole also takes in what spills out of its control (the profile photo over the cover). A control that goes away, or a dialog opening over the page, pauses the chapter.
- **Targets**: `data-tour` ids on the real controls — `home-composer`, `home-mode`, `home-examples`, `home-get` (Home); `nav-*`, `plan`, `account`, `wallet`, `support-tutorial`, `support-tour`, `menu` (sidebar and phone bar); `account-level` (the account menu); `page-head` (every `PageHeader`), `profile-head`, `plan-credits`, `balance-card`; `chat-question`, `chat-concept`, `chat-build`, `chat-save` (the chat); `project-tabs`, `project-product`, `project-earn`, `project-actions` (project page), `product-editor` (a product's Open in editor); `rail-pcb` … `rail-preview` (the editors' rails), `pcb-properties`, `pcb-price`, `editor-saved`, `editor-help`; `earn-path`, `earn-step-mint` / `-sell`, `earn-overview` / `-sell` / `-plan` / `-money` (Earn's tabs), `earn-sell-rules`, `earn-sales` (`EarnBlock`'s `tour`); `feed-modes`, `feed-categories`, `feed-card` (Innovations), `post-media`, `post-head` (a post). The first one on screen counts. The sidebar's footer panels stay open when the tour card or scrim is pressed.
- **Tour Guide panel** (`tour-guide-panel.tsx`): Support › Tour Guide opens it beside the sidebar (the footer popovers' `FloatingPanel`); ⌘K "Open Tour Guide" and the phone's menu open it as a dialog (a sheet on a phone). The chapters under **Start**, **Build** and **Earn & share**: each row is one quiet button — a status mark (✓ seen, ▶ part-way, ○ not yet), the name, its steps and place, and **Start**, **Replay** or **Resume n/N** with an arrow — or, when it can't start yet, a lock, why ("Make your first project to open this.", "Send your first idea from Home to see these.", "Open IDEEZA on a computer to see the editors.") with **Go to Home** where that helps and Home isn't already open (D6). Then **Offer a tour the first time I open a page** (the DS Toggle) and **Reset tours**, which clears it all and brings the Welcome back on Home. A chapter started from another page goes to its first step's page: Home, the project on screen or the one changed last (its product, editors and Earn), the latest chat (tips are turned on and show there), Innovations. (The journey row the plan had as D7 was taken out at the owner's request, 2026-10-22.)
- **Record**: `ideeza:tour` (`lib/tour/state.ts`, read live by `use-tour.ts`) — the Welcome's answer, each chapter's state (done, skipped, paused) and step, whether first-visit offers are on, and the offers answered with Not now. Which page is which, the offers' and tips' rules and each chapter's readiness are `lib/tour/places.ts`.
- **Ways in**: the Welcome, Support › Tour Guide (live; Help Center is still "not available yet"), ⌘K › Resources › Open Tour Guide.
