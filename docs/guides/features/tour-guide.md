## Tour Guide (`components/tour`, `lib/tour`)
- **Welcome** (`welcome-dialog.tsx`, DS M07 Modal): on Home, once, to a browser with no projects and no chats (or after Reset tours). The path from idea to product in five lines; **Show me around** runs Start here, **I'll explore on my own** (or ✕, Escape, the scrim) closes it for good and says where the tour lives. A bottom sheet on a phone.
- **Chapters** (`lib/tour/chapters.ts`), the Figma's Dashboard Homepage Tour in parts of at most five steps:
  - **Start here** (Home): the prompt box, With AI or by hand, examples and Enhance, what every build gives, My projects.
  - **Find your way** (sidebar): Home, History, Parts & agile module, Explore marketplace + Innovations, Blog + Messages.
  - **Your account** (sidebar footer): profile, credits (the prices read from `CONCEPT_COST` / `BUILD_COST`), level, wallet, Tutorial.
  - **From idea to concepts** (a concept chat), **tips** rather than steps: each shows the moment its part of the chat is on screen — the questions (once they are asked, not while the AI reads), the first finished concept card, Build (its price from `BUILD_COST`), Save Project — with **Got it** and **Turn off tips**. Using the control under a tip (answering, pressing Build) counts it as seen; a tip never opens while someone is typing. On for a browser's first chat, or when turned on from the panel.
  - **Your project** (`/projects/<id>`): the tab strip, a product card, Earn, the ⋯ menu.
  - **The editors** (any editor module, the Figma's Pro pages tour): the rail's PCB Design, Code, 3D Module, Assembly (the Figma's missing 5/8), Product Preview.
  - **Inside the PCB editor**: the Properties panel, the premium parts price ($0.00 while every library part is free), the save status, Help.
  - On a phone the sidebar is a drawer, so Find your way and Your account are one step each on the menu button, and Start here's last step points there too. The editors' chapters aren't offered on a phone.
- **First-visit offer** (`chapter-offer.tsx`, spec D2): the first time a project page or an editor opens, an M10 Popover in the corner (above the editors' status bar) offers its chapter — **Show me** or **Not now** — without dimming the page or taking focus. The editors before Inside the PCB editor; one offer per chapter, never again after Not now or once started; off with the panel's switch.
- **A step** (`tour-step.tsx`): the page under the app's dialog scrim with the step's controls cut out (a hole, so the control itself still works), and the M10 Popover beside them, placed by `@floating-ui/dom` (flip, shift, arrow) and kept there through scrolls and resizes: "n/N" and **Skip Tour Guide**, title, body, **Previous** (from step 2) and **Next Tip**; the last step says **Explore**, with "Next part: … →" to the next chapter. Each step takes focus on its title and is announced ("Step 2 of 5: …"). ← → move, Escape skips; keys typed in the popup stop there. A control that isn't on screen is waited for (6 s for a run's first step, which may be waiting on its page; 1.5 s after), then skipped; a control that goes away, or a dialog opening over the page, pauses the chapter.
- **Targets**: `data-tour` ids on the real controls — `home-composer`, `home-mode`, `home-examples`, `home-get` (Home); `nav-*`, `plan`, `account`, `wallet`, `support-tutorial`, `support-tour`, `menu` (sidebar and phone bar); `chat-question`, `chat-concept`, `chat-build`, `chat-save` (the chat); `project-tabs`, `project-product`, `project-earn`, `project-actions` (project page); `rail-pcb` … `rail-preview` (the editors' rails), `pcb-properties`, `pcb-price`, `editor-saved`, `editor-help`. The first one on screen counts.
- **Tour Guide panel** (`tour-guide-panel.tsx`): Support › Tour Guide opens it beside the sidebar (the footer popovers' `FloatingPanel`); ⌘K "Open Tour Guide" and the phone's menu open it as a dialog (a sheet on a phone). The chapters under **Start** and **Build**, each with its steps and place, ✓ Seen, and **Start**, **Replay** or **Resume n/N** — or, when it can't start yet, why ("Make your first project to open this.", "Send your first idea from Home to see these.", "Open IDEEZA on a computer to see the editors.") with **Go to Home** where that helps (D6). Then **Offer a tour the first time I open a page** (the DS Toggle) and **Reset tours**, which clears it all and brings the Welcome back on Home. A chapter started from another page goes to its page first: Home, the project on screen or the one changed last, that project's PCB editor, the latest chat (tips are turned on and show there).
- **Record**: `ideeza:tour` (`lib/tour/state.ts`, read live by `use-tour.ts`) — the Welcome's answer, each chapter's state (done, skipped, paused) and step, whether first-visit offers are on, and the offers answered with Not now. Which page is which, the offers' and tips' rules and each chapter's readiness are `lib/tour/places.ts`.
- **Ways in**: the Welcome, Support › Tour Guide (live; Help Center is still "not available yet"), ⌘K › Resources › Open Tour Guide.
