## Tour Guide (`components/tour`, `lib/tour`)

### The map — the Welcome (`welcome-dialog.tsx`, DS M07 Modal)
On Home, to a browser with no projects and no chats (or after Reset tours): the whole platform on one screen — "Turn an idea into a real electronic product, with AI or by hand. No electronics knowledge needed.", the starting credits read from the credits store ("You have 18 free credits to start. A concept uses 1."), and the path as five stages on one thread (Describe your idea · See your concept · Build it · Make it yours · Own it, sell it or share it). **Start with my idea** (primary) opens the first-product guide with the cursor in the prompt box; **Look around first** runs the Full tour of IDEEZA (owner, 2026-10-26); the link **I know hardware — start by hand** switches Home to Build manually and puts the focus on its Create Project (not on a phone). ✕, Escape or the scrim close it for this visit: it comes back once on a later visit (a new tab or window), then counts as declined, and the first close points at where the tours live. A bottom sheet on a phone, with "On a phone you can send ideas, follow builds, sell and share. Editing a design needs a computer."

### The first-product guide (`lib/tour/guide.ts`, `guide-card.tsx`, spec D17)
A card that stays with a new user on every page until their first product is made: "Your first product", "n of 8", the DS A22 progress bar, what to do now, and **Show me** (where it happens: rings the control and puts the focus on it) or **Take me there** (anywhere else: opens that page, then rings it). Its 8 steps — **Write your idea** (Home's prompt box) · **Answer the AI's question** · **See your concept** · **Press Build** · **Save your project** · **Open it in the editor** (a product's page; left out on a phone, so 7 there) · **Mint it** (Earn's mint step) · **Share it** (the project's ⋯ › Share to Innovations) — move on by themselves from the app's own state (the newest chat's question, its concepts and build, the projects' `editorOpened`, `mint` and `showcasedAt`), never a Next button; a project made by hand counts for everything up to the save. While the app works (reading the idea, drawing, building) the card says so with a spinner and the user can look around. A new step, or the end of a wait, rings its control once it is on screen, without moving the page or the focus. It sits in the foot corner (above the editors' status bar; along the foot on a phone), under dialogs, never dims or takes the focus, and rises to sit just above the step's control when it would cover it. – folds it into a pill with the count; ✕ closes it and points once at where the tours live. It hides under a chapter, the panel and the Welcome, and on the mint flow and the brief. While it is up the chat's and the editors' tips, visit offers and the resume card wait. The last card: "Your first product is done" → **Full tour of IDEEZA** / Close. Up next's Write your first idea and Back to your build open it too, until it is done.

### The path — Up next (`nextFor`, `lib/tour/places.ts`)
Every chapter's last card hands the user the next real step on their path, worked out from what they have done (the chapter just finished counts as seen): no chat or project yet → **Write your first idea** (Home, the cursor in the prompt box); a chat but nothing saved → **Back to your build**; then **Next: Your project** → **Next: The editors** (never on a phone) → **Next: Mint and sell** → **Mint your project** (Earn, while not minted) → **Next: Share with the community** → the side tours (Find your way, Your account) → nothing ("You have seen the whole path"). It crosses the panel's groups; a part that can't run here (readiness) is never offered. The full tour seen to the end counts its parts as seen. The same line opens the Tour Guide panel ("Up next") once the first product is made.

### The chapters (`lib/tour/chapters.ts`), in the order of the path
Every step names its page (`page`, a `PageRef`): it opens that page first, then lights the step there; a step without one runs where its chapter's place is (the editors' map in any module). Copy reads the app's own numbers (`CONCEPT_COST`, `IDEEZA_FEE_BPS`); no step types a build price (What to build sets it).
- **Full tour of IDEEZA** (spec D18, first in the panel): every page one by one in the sidebar's order — Home (Start here's 3), History, My projects, then the project's own pages (Your project's 5, the editors' rail, each module's page, the save status and Finish, Mint and sell's 5), Parts, Marketplace, the feed and a post, Blog, Messages, then Your account's 5. Made of the parts' own steps (34); without a project its project pages are left out (16), on a phone the editors and the level. Never offered on a visit.
- **Start here** (Home, 3 steps): the prompt box (a concept's cost), With AI or by hand (Build manually for someone who knows electronics), examples and Enhance. Its Up next writes the first idea.
- **From idea to concepts** (the chat), **tips in order**: the choices (nothing is charged until Start drawing), the concept (one per product; Refine or Regenerate), **What to build** (the pieces card: credits, time, Edit, the free parts list), Build (you can leave; the bell tells you), Save (name it, then View project).
- **Your project** (5): the tabs, **Add a product**, Earn, the ⋯ menu, then a product's page for **Open in editor**.
- **The editors** (3, without hopping between modules): the rail in order ("AI already made each one"), the save status, **Finish** (in Product Preview: on to mint).
- **Editor tips** (tips in any order, the Figma's Pro pages 1–5): one per module, the first time that module opens, after The editors was taken or answered; never on a phone.
- **Inside the PCB editor** (4, the task in order): the Library tab and **Place a Part…** (the step opens the tab), the Schematic | PCB switch (the board is made from the schematic; Auto Route, DRC), Properties, Help › Tutorials. The Figma's Pro 7/8 (premium parts price) is the price chip's DS A19 tooltip (hover or focus).
- **Mint and sell** (5): Overview's two steps and its Mint (an NFT; a free testnet demo; the demo wallet signs), Sell (the whole project or each product's copies, each listed on its own; the fee), **Business plan** (no mint or wallet needed), Money (your share after the fee and splits joins one balance; withdraw in Settings › Get paid).
- **Share with the community** (3): the feed's modes and categories, the first post, your project's ⋯ › Share to Innovations (left out until there is a project).
- **Find your way** (side tour, 6): My projects (Figma 11/19), History, Parts, Explore marketplace, Messages, Blog — each page opened, its row and page header lit together.
- **Your account** (side tour, 5): profile, credits (Plan & pricing's Credits tab), level (the account menu, opened and closed by the step; left out on a phone), wallet, Tutorials (their videos will pay IDZ once they are out).

### Help where you are
- **Tips** (`tips` on a chapter): the same card as a step but they ring their control **without dimming the page or taking the focus**, leave Escape to the page, and close as seen once their control is pressed (a list it opens is never covered). Got it / Turn off tips. The chat's are on for a browser's first chat (or from the panel); the editor modules' as above.
- **Visit offer** (`chapter-offer.tsx`, `CornerCard`, spec D2): the first time a project page, an editor, Earn or Innovations opens, "A quick tour of this page · {chapter} · n steps" with **Show me** / **Not now** — no dimming, no focus. Not now once waits for a later visit (`snoozed` + the visit memory, `visit.ts`); twice, never again (`offered`). The panel's switch turns offers off.
- **Resume card**: a part left half-way on an earlier visit comes back once, on a page where it can run ("Pick up where you left off · {chapter} · step n of N" → Resume / Not now; Not now leaves it in the panel).
- **Arrival card**: someone who opens a shared post, listing or creator profile and has made nothing here yet is told "This project was made on IDEEZA, where anyone turns an idea into a real electronic product." → **Make your own** (Home, where the Welcome waits) / Look around. Once.
- **Where the tours live**: the first time the user closes the Welcome, skips, says Not now, turns tips off or finishes with Done for now, one card points at Support › Tour Guide (the menu button on a phone): "Tours live here" / Got it. Once.

### The card (`tour-step.tsx`)
One layer for a whole chapter. The page sits under the app's dialog scrim (an SVG path) with the step's controls cut out — a rounded hole 6 px clear of them that follows the control's corners, ringed in the focus colour; the control itself still works. A 320 px card (DS surface, border, radius lg, Elevation 3) with M10's arrow, placed by `@floating-ui/dom`: "n of N" and **Skip tour** (on the last card **Done for now**), the title, the body, **Previous** (ghost) and **Next** (primary, with an arrow); the last card's primary is **Up next**, or **Done** when nothing is next. On a phone the card sits along the foot of the screen. Motion: fade in once; between steps the hole and the card glide together (300 ms, the standard curve) while the words cross-fade; an ending fades out; reduced motion makes every change immediate. Each step takes focus on its title and is announced; when a tour ends with nothing to give the focus back to, the page's h1 takes it. ← → move, Escape skips. A step on another page navigates there first: the card stays and already shows the next step while the old page's holes close, then glides to the new control; presses meanwhile are ignored. Without a project, steps about a project's pages are left out (`runnableSteps`), as are `desktopOnly` steps on a phone, so the count never jumps. A move that lands elsewhere pauses the chapter. A control that isn't on screen is waited for (6 s after a page change or for a run's first step; 1.5 s after), then skipped; a step with two targets waits briefly for both. A step with `reveal` presses that control when a target isn't there (the account menu for the level, closed again after; the Credits tab; the PCB editor's Library tab). The tour layer sits one level above popovers (`z-toast`). A page that redraws a control is followed to the new one on the same page; a control gone for good (600 ms), a change of address, or a dialog opening pauses the chapter.

### Targets
`data-tour` ids on the real controls — Home: `home-composer`, `home-mode`, `home-examples`, `home-create` (Build manually's Create Project); sidebar: `nav-*`, `plan`, `account`, `account-level`, `wallet`, `support-tutorial`, `support-tour`, `menu`; pages: `page-head` (every `PageHeader`), `profile-head`, `plan-credits`, `plan-credits-tab`, `balance-card`; chat: `chat-question`, `chat-concept`, `chat-pick` (What to build), `chat-build`, `chat-save`; project: `project-tabs`, `project-add`, `project-product`, `project-earn`, `project-actions`, `product-editor`; editors: `editor-rail` (each rail), `rail-*`, `editor-saved`, `editor-finish`, `editor-help`, `pcb-library-tab`, `pcb-place`, `pcb-mode`, `pcb-properties`, `pcb-price`; Earn: `earn-path`, `earn-step-*`, `earn-{tab}`, `earn-sell-rules`, `earn-plan-doc`, `earn-sales`; Innovations: `feed-modes`, `feed-categories`, `feed-card`, `post-media`, `post-head`.

### Tour Guide panel (`tour-guide-panel.tsx`)
Support › Tour Guide opens it beside the sidebar; ⌘K "Open Tour Guide", the phone's menu and every editor's **Help › Tour Guide…** open it as a dialog. First, until the first product is made, **Your first product**: "n of 8", the progress bar, "Now: {step}" (or "A guide stays with you until your first product is made.") and **Start the guide** / **Show the guide** / **Show the guide again**; after that **Up next** (one line). Then the chapters, the full tour first, under Start, Build, Earn & share and Look around, each counting the steps that will really run here: a status mark (seen / part-way / not yet), the name, its steps and place, and Start, Replay or Resume n/N — or, when it can't start yet, why ("Opens once you save your first project.", "These tips show in your first AI chat. Write an idea to start one.", "Open IDEEZA on a computer to see the editors.") with Go to Home where that helps. Then **Offer a tour the first time I open a page** (DS Toggle) and **Reset tours**.

### Every editor's Help
PCB (its schematic, 2D and 3D menus), Code, 3D Module, and Assembly / Wiring / Preview (`EditorHelpMenu`) each list **Tutorials** (the module's own topic, in a new tab so the editor stays), **Tour of this editor** (The editors; Inside the PCB editor in PCB), **Tour Guide…** and Report a problem.

### Record
`ideeza:tour` (`lib/tour/state.ts`, read live by `use-tour.ts`): the Welcome's answer (unseen, started, later, declined), each chapter's state (done, skipped, paused), step and, for any-order tips, the ones seen; whether offers are on; offers snoozed once and offers declined for good; whether where the tours live has been pointed out; whether the arrival card was shown; the first-product guide (off, open, small, closed, done) — its step is never stored, it is read from the app. What was turned down on this visit lives in `sessionStorage` (`components/tour/visit.ts`). Which page is which, the offers' and tips' rules, readiness and Up next are `lib/tour/places.ts`.

### Ways in
The Welcome (Start with my idea → the guide), the guide's card and pill, Support › Tour Guide, ⌘K › Resources › Open Tour Guide, every editor's Help, a visit offer, the resume card, the arrival card.
